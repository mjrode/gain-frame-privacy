## Positioning

- **Web tagline (hero):** "You're more than the scale"
- **Web subheadline:** "Track the measurements and photos that matter. Stay motivated on your weight-loss journey."
- **Developer:** LASMIT TLB LTD (UK)
- **Primary CTA:** "Download on the App Store"
- **Target audience signals:** **Weight loss audience.** "Stay motivated on your weight-loss journey" is the explicit framing. Tens of thousands of users since 2012 have "reached their weight-loss goals."

### Critical positioning insight

**Progress is NOT positioned for serious gym-goers.** The hero ("You're more than the scale") and subhead frame this as a weight-loss app. Their blog content reinforces it: how-to lose weight, mindful eating, meal prep, sleep, "BJ Dropped 44lbs in 8 Weeks" testimonials, "How to Survive the Easter Egg Hunt — Weight Loss Tips."

This is a **broader, more weight-loss-focused audience than GainFrame**. They're competing for the same downloads (anyone tracking body changes via photos) but speaking to a different segment.

## Notes for comparison article writing

If `comparison-article-generator` ever writes "Progress (Lasmit) vs GainFrame":

- **Verified pricing:** Free + one-time IAP unlock. Per-feature unlock model — different from GainFrame's subscription. Specific Pro price needs verification.
- **Verified accuracy claim:** **None — they don't measure body composition.** They measure tape-measurement-based body fat and BMI calculations from height/weight inputs. Their BMI / body fat calculators are formula-based, not AI vision-based.
- **Verified target audience:** Weight-loss focused. Broader fitness audience including those just starting their journey, not gym-trained lifters.
- **Known limitations (from missing features):**
  - No AI body composition from photo
  - No muscle group scoring
  - No Future Physique prediction
  - No workout integration (Apple Health + Fitbit only — no Hevy / Strong / etc.)
  - No camera roll batch import with auto-pose classification
  - Photo comparison is manual side-by-side, not AI-powered
- **Verified strengths to acknowledge (very strong list):**
  - **15,000 reviews @ 4.6 stars** — most credible competitor in the top 6
  - **14-year track record** since 2012 — proven reliability, no abandonment risk
  - 17 preset + 10 custom body measurements — more granular tape-measure tracking than GainFrame has
  - Both Apple Health AND Fitbit integration (GainFrame is Apple Health only)
  - Free BMI + body fat calculator pages (web tool moat)
  - "Tens of thousands of weight-loss success stories" — narrative authority
- **Honest framing:** Progress and GainFrame serve different users:
  - **Progress wins for:** people on weight-loss journeys (not gain), users who want tape-measure tracking + photos, Fitbit users, people wary of subscription-only pricing, users prioritizing brand longevity / proven reliability.
  - **GainFrame wins for:** serious lifters (not weight-loss), users who want AI body comp from photos (not manual entry), users who want per-muscle scoring + Future Physique, users on Hevy.
- **Comparison article angle suggestion:** "Progress vs GainFrame: Which body tracking app fits your goal?" — angle on **goal alignment** (weight loss vs muscle gain), not feature parity. Acknowledge Progress's social proof and longevity honestly.

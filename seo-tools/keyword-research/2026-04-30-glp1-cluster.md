## Cluster Goal

GLP-1 medications (Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, Zepbound, semaglutide, tirzepatide) are the highest-growth weight-loss audience of 2024-2026. Per the [2026-04-30 competitor refresh](../competitor-research/_identified-2026-04-30.md), **SKOR is the first competitor explicitly targeting this audience with a body-tracking app** — they're claiming a category that should be ours. GainFrame's value prop (track BF% + lean mass + muscle group scores from photos) is *uniquely* positioned for GLP-1 users because muscle loss is the #1 documented side effect, and DEXA scans are too expensive to do monthly.

This cluster carves out the **gym-native + body-composition-aware** sub-niche of the GLP-1 audience. We are NOT trying to compete on pure medical queries (those are Cleveland Clinic / WebMD / Healthline lockout territory). We're competing on the visual progress + body comp tracking sub-niche where our existing authority + tooling actually wins.

## Strategic Notes

**SKOR is the named competitor for this cluster.** Their positioning is "the only app that scores your skin, body, and GLP-1 progress together." They're claiming the GLP-1 tracking category before anyone else. Our competitive answer is GainFrame's stronger photo + body composition + DEXA-validated angle WITHOUT the skincare/face-tracking dilution. Honest co-mention: include SKOR in the listicle (#4) as the GLP-1-specific option but rank GainFrame higher on body comp depth.

**The audience overlap is bigger than it looks.** Many GLP-1 users come from gym-adjacent backgrounds — they tried diet/exercise, hit a plateau, and now want a tool. They WILL still lift while on the medication if a coach tells them to (and most coaches do). Our gym-native voice is an asset here, not a liability. Don't pretend GLP-1 users aren't real lifters.

**Don't make medical claims.** Every post should explicitly note "this is not medical advice, talk to your doctor about the medication itself." Our authority is on the *tracking + body composition* side, not on the *medical decision to take GLP-1*.

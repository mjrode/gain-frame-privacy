## Methodology + Honesty Notes

- **GSC export** (May-11): 1,000 queries, 4,673 impressions, 397 clicks. Filtered to seed-relevant subset (93 queries) for direct analysis.
- **Autocomplete depth:** 19 parallel WebFetch calls to `suggestqueries.google.com` covering base + question + variant prefixes. Pulled ~150 raw suggestions, deduped + filtered to 35.
- **SERP analysis:** 24 WebSearch calls covering all 27 net-new candidates + 7 GSC-anchored queries (with the long-tail GSC queries skipped because their action is title fix, not difficulty assessment).
- **Difficulty heuristic:** start at 30, +20 per Wikipedia in top 5, +15 per tier-1 health authority in top 10, +10 per major fitness brand, −10 per Reddit/Quora, −15 per small blog. Clamped 0-100.
- **Volume tier:** High = 8+ autocomplete suggestions + PAA present; Med = 4-7 or PAA; Low = neither. GSC impressions used as confirmation signal when available.
- **No paid API.** Volume figures are estimates. Validate top picks in Ahrefs/SEMrush before committing 8+ hours to a single post.

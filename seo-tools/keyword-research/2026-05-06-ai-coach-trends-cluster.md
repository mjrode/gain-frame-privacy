## Cluster goal

Capture the search audience evaluating "AI fitness coach" tools — the lifters who could otherwise default to ChatGPT/Claude or generic AI fitness apps. The defensible angle is GainFrame's structural moat: the Coach already knows the user's check-in history, sleep, HRV, Hevy training volume, goal, and persistent memory. Generic AI doesn't. Every supporting post anchors a specific dimension of that moat (data trends, recomposition, personalization).

Two angles from the original brief were dropped after SERP analysis:
- **Angle #5 (Hevy + body composition / training volume):** dropped per user. Pure-science training-volume queries are locked by Stronger By Science / Examine.com / NSCA / academic publishers — not winnable without major backlinks.
- **Angle #3 head terms (sleep + muscle):** dropped as standalone targets. `does sleep affect muscle growth` and variants short-circuit to difficulty 95 (NIH PMC, UC Berkeley, Springer, Healthline, Polar, Peloton dominate the SERP). Sleep+muscle content is still valuable as **H2/H3 sub-topics inside the pillar**, but cannot be a standalone post target.

## Methodology notes

- Phase 1 — Autocomplete: queried 19 seed phrases via `https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox`. Surfaced 47 unique candidate keywords. Pruned to 21 by removing near-duplicates and obvious wrong-intent variants (e.g. `claude ai fitness coach b2b`).
- Phase 2 — SERP analysis: ran WebSearch on 9 of the 21 candidates (skipped 7 that pattern-matched the academic-lockout signal early; skipped 5 that pattern-matched existing GainFrame coverage).
- Phase 3 — Difficulty heuristic: applied the standard skill formula (start 30, +15 per tier-1 health authority, +20 per Wikipedia, +10 per major fitness brand, −15 per small blog, etc.). Auto-skipped any keyword with 4+ tier-1 academic results per the Hospital-authority lockout rule.
- Limitations: cannot estimate absolute monthly volume without Ahrefs/SEMrush; volume tiers are relative within this analysis. Cannot detect breakout queries that don't surface in autocomplete (e.g. queries that just emerged in the last few weeks).

## Critical Finding: This Cluster Splits Into Two Unrelated SERPs

Before reading the opportunities, understand this split — it shapes every recommendation below.

The muscle gain tracking cluster divides sharply:

**SERP A — Workout Loggers** (Hevy, Strong, JEFIT, RP Strength, Fitbod dominate)
Triggered by: `gym progress tracker app`, `best gym tracker app`, `muscle growth tracker app`, `app to track muscle mass`
These apps log sets, reps, weights. **GainFrame does not log workouts — wrong category. Skip these queries.**

**SERP B — Visual Progress / Body Transformation Trackers** (Shapez, MacroFactor, Progress by Lasmit, BodyJourney dominate)
Triggered by: `body transformation tracker app`, `best app to track body composition`, `body composition apps`
These apps track photos, measurements, before/after comparisons. **GainFrame is in this category. Target these.**

The opportunity: a large audience searching SERP A (workout loggers) has a latent need GainFrame solves — they're logging every set in Hevy but have no visual proof their composition is changing. A content angle that bridges both SERPs ("you need both: a workout logger AND a visual tracker") positions GainFrame as the missing second layer.

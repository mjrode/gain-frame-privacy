## Evidence and limitations

- Baseline: 241 articles; after this batch: 251. Rechecked the live filesystem before writing.
- Current GSC final window: August 8 through September 4, 2026. Inspected all 31,293 returned page/query rows across the primary and continuation exports.
- Eight exact queries have no returned page/query row. `zozofit review` has 2 impressions and 0 clicks on the alternatives roundup; `evolt 360 body scan accuracy` has 1 impression and 0 clicks on the general body-fat chart. These are too small to establish stable ownership. Missing rows are not proof of zero demand or zero overlap, because GSC omits some queries.
- Title/description token-overlap check: zero new flagged pairs. The one existing pair, average-bicep-size versus average-chest-size, remains unchanged. This is a screening heuristic, not proof that Google will never rank two URLs for the same query.
- Every new article receives three contextual inbound links from existing articles. Thirty new contextual edges come from sixteen source articles.
- New metadata names only the specific product or comparison intent. Each page has its own canonical, five matching FAQs, a 45-50-word Quick Answer (recounted after final editing), an original generated cover, and a practical table or worksheet.
- Four app figures are publisher-supplied App Store images, downloaded from current US iTunes records and labeled as promotional examples. No invented screenshots or results. Other covers are abstract illustrations, not hardware photographs.
- Existing Hume and RENPHO individual review files are untouched. Their prior measurement windows cannot be treated as isolated experiments after adding an adjacent comparison and links. Keep the September 22 Hume review check, but annotate this publication as an intervening change and do not claim an uncontaminated causal result.

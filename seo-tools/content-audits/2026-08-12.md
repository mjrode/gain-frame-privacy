## Executive answer: does the advice contradict the strategy?

Mostly no. It confirms that the progress-photo job is GainFrame's strongest reachable App Store
lane and that the broad word `progress` is not worth chasing. It also confirms the decision to
protect the current title.

Two parts need correction:

1. **“A small metadata change should get `progress photo tracker` to #1” is not supported.** The
   live title already contains `Gym Progress Photos`, and the live/staged subtitle contains
   `Tracker`. The exact phrase is already covered across the two highest-weight visible fields.
   GainFrame was #4 in the reproducible Apple Search API proxy on 2026-07-21 and is #6 now in both
   the supplied scan and a fresh API check. Metadata inclusion is not the missing piece, and the
   two-rank slide means #1 should not be promised from a copy tweak alone.
2. **`body measurement tracker` is a product-intent mismatch today.** GainFrame estimates body
   composition from photos but does not log chest, waist, hip, arm, or thigh circumference. The
   live Google SERP is dominated by tape-measure logs and dedicated measurement apps. Targeting
   the phrase visibly would create an expectation the product does not meet.

`fitness progress tracker` also remains the broad workout-log lane the prior ASO audit rejected:
Hevy, Strong, JEFIT, Google Fit, StrengthLog, and fitness templates own the live Google SERP.

## Live SERP briefs

### `progress photo tracker` — observed 2026-08-12, US, English — **fix existing / no new page**

- **Intent and format:** mixed app-discovery and recommendation intent. Google rewards Reddit
  recommendations, App Store/Google Play listings, product feature pages, and one roundup.
- **Leading results:** Reddit r/MacroFactor #1; Apple's Progress Body Tracker listing #2; Google
  Play Body Tracker #3; GainFrame's `best-free-progress-photo-apps` #4; Metamorph #5; Hevy #6;
  MacroFactor #7. GainFrame already meets the winning roundup format.
- **SERP features:** asynchronous AI Overview, PAA, related searches. PAA asks for the best tracker,
  free options, photo-taking method, and cadence.
- **Coverage and visual standard:** storage, alignment, side-by-side comparison, measurements,
  privacy, and free-vs-paid choice. Marketplace cards and product screenshots set the visual bar.
- **Unique GainFrame contribution:** the existing roundup explains storage/compare choices and the
  product adds AI body-composition analysis, Smart Import, and no-tripod alignment. A new page would
  duplicate the existing winner.

### `body measurement tracker` — observed 2026-08-12, US, English — **kill for current product**

- **Intent and format:** transactional manual-measurement logging. Google rewards app listings,
  measurement tools, templates, and tape-measure tracking guides.
- **Leading results:** Google Play Body Measurement & Weight Loss #1; My Body Measurement Tracker
  on the App Store #2; Reddit #3; BiteKit measurement logger #4; Progress Body Tracker #5.
- **SERP features:** PAA and related searches, no AI Overview in this pull. Recurring tasks are
  entering chest, waist, hip, arm, and thigh measurements and charting them over time.
- **Unique GainFrame contribution:** none that satisfies the primary job today. The existing
  `best-body-scanning-measurement-apps` page is honest that GainFrame does not log circumferences
  and already surfaced at position ~2 on the exact query, directionally, with 11 page-level
  impressions. Do not turn that incidental visibility into a product promise.

### `fitness progress tracker` — observed 2026-08-12, US, English — **kill**

- **Intent and format:** workout logging, activity tracking, strength progress, and templates.
- **Leading results:** Hevy #1; Strong #2; Notion fitness templates #3; Hevy homepage #4; Google Fit
  #5; StrengthLog #6; JEFIT #7; Zapier roundup #8.
- **SERP features:** image pack, PAA, and related searches. Recurring coverage is sets, reps,
  routines, activity, and workout charts.
- **Unique GainFrame contribution:** visual body change is adjacent, but it does not replace a
  workout logger. The Hevy integration supports a complementary story, not ownership of this term.

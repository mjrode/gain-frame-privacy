## Productizable decision record

The reusable logic is a gated decision system, not a “highest volume wins” score:

1. **Snapshot the evidence.** Every metric stores source, property/project, exact date range,
   freshness, and metric definition. GSC, GA4, PostHog, and DataForSEO are never merged into one
   unnamed traffic number.
2. **Generate candidates.** Candidates come from striking-distance queries, decaying pages,
   competitor gaps, topical-map gaps, local hygiene defects, and previously scheduled decisions.
3. **Apply hard gates before scoring.** Reject or delay candidates with unreliable GSC samples,
   intent/product mismatch, an existing page that already owns the query, an active measurement
   window, unhealthy indexing, no business job, or missing current market data.
4. **Choose the action type.** If an existing page owns intent, choose `fix_existing`. If a blog
   and tool split intent correctly, choose `bridge_to_tool`. If evidence is too early, choose
   `watch_until(date)`. Only a clean intent gap with a conversion or support job can become `write`.
5. **Score survivors on separate axes.** Keep search opportunity (volume, impressions, position,
   trend), business value (activation or conversion evidence), feasibility (KD versus site
   ceiling, SERP format, proprietary assets), and risk (cannibalization, zero-click, YMYL,
   experiment collision) visible instead of hiding them in one opaque number.
6. **Persist reasons and supersession.** A decision stores `reason_codes`, evidence references,
   `blocked_until`, comparable pages, confidence, and which older decision it supersedes. That is
   how the system avoids re-proposing the same idea or acting on stale state.

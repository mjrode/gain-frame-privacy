### P1 — ZOZOFIT review

- **Observed:** Aug 23, 2026; Google US/en desktop through DataForSEO live SERP.
- **Intent and format:** current product review, accuracy check, cost, and worth-it decision. The
  result set is unusually open: forums, App Store/Google Play, the official site, Trustpilot, and
  older video/suit reviews dominate instead of a current independent app-only written review.
- **Leading URLs and freshness:** [ZOZOFIT's current product page](https://zozofit.com/), the
  [US App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/zozofit-3d-body-scanner/id1636398776),
  [Trustpilot reviews](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/zozofit.com), and a July 2026
  [ZOZOSUIT review](https://gadgetsandwearables.com/2023/07/01/zozosuit-review/). The independent
  written coverage is still suit-centric while the current product supports app-only scanning.
- **SERP features:** AI Overview, People Also Ask, discussions/forums, and video results.
- **Recurring coverage:** scan accuracy and repeatability, tape comparison, free versus Premium,
  current app-only versus optional suit workflow, setup friction, body-fat interpretation, and
  whether it is worth paying for.
- **Visual standard:** real setup/output screenshots and a controlled repeatability table. Generic
  stock art or screenshots copied from the store would not clear the result set's evidence bar.
- **Unique GainFrame contribution:** three same-session app-only scans under fixed clothing,
  lighting, posture, and phone placement; tape measurements at the same body sites; observed setup
  failures; current pricing/workflow screenshots; and a use-case verdict from a progress-photo
  product team. ZOZOFIT's current official plan is free for core scans, with Premium at $4.99/mo,
  $29.99/yr, or $99.99 lifetime; the optional suit includes lifetime Premium.
- **Decision:** write only after the first-party evidence pack exists. Without it, hold.

## Productizable decision record

feature's existence is not search demand. If the whole feature-aligned batch returns no data, use
existing product education or a feature landing page for user support; do not manufacture a blog
keyword. A new competitor review survives only when its intent is distinct from the existing
alternatives/hub pages and GainFrame can contribute first-party evidence that the live SERP lacks.

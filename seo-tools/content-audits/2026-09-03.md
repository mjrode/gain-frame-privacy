### Why these formats

- **WeightWatchers AI Body Scanner:** the live results are led by WeightWatchers, Reddit,
  Facebook, and app listings. Only one independent written review appeared in the first page.
  The secondary query `weight watchers ai body scanner` adds 110 searches at KD 5. The opening
  exists, but a generic summary would not be competitive.
- **InBody vs DEXA:** the results mix Reddit, provider guides, and a study, with no single dominant
  consumer explanation. The post should compare repeatability, output, price, radiation, and what
  to do when the readings disagree. GainFrame's existing DEXA benchmark supplies a credible bridge.
- **Evolt 360:** current results lean toward the manufacturer and local gyms. Independent
  interpretation is thin, but experience evidence is a prerequisite.
- **DEXA body-fat percentage:** the market is unusually large and growing, but the results include
  medical and institutional authorities. This should be treated as a flagship evidence asset,
  not a quick SEO article.
- **Body-fat calipers:** the ranking set is product-heavy and includes Amazon, Reddit, research,
  and established fitness publishers. A hands-on guide is the only defensible format.

### Do not create these sibling pages

- `body measurements for weight loss` belongs in the existing measurement guide.
- `shoulder to hip ratio` belongs in the existing shoulder-to-waist page, which already ranks.
- `torso to leg ratio` belongs in the long-torso/short-legs owner.
- `waist vs hip measurement men` belongs in the waist-to-hip-ratio owner.
- `average calf size` belongs in the average-thigh-size owner.

**Only two posts have real evidence this run, so only two are proposed.** The count is a ceiling,
not a quota. Proposing eight more would mean writing into clusters that DataForSEO shows shedding
19–66% of volume per year — the November version of this audit would then be explaining why the
new posts underperformed.

**The honest recommendation is to run fixes F1–F5 first and publish nothing.** The site has 46
orphaned posts and no indexing constraint; connecting what already exists will move more traffic
than two new posts, and it establishes a clean measurement window for the next cycle.

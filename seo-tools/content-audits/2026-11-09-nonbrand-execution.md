## Release checks

The production candidate was assembled from the committed site and the selected 26 changed or new files. The full build rendered 597 routes. Strict checks passed on all 13 changed articles and the 293-post inventory passed. The four new articles each have a canonical URL, a single H1, five matching visible/structured FAQ answers, and two distinct in-body links from established articles. The two numbered protocols have matching instructions in structured data. The arm and training-break guides each link to the photo comparison tool within the article body, including when the site varies the closing card.

The three one-page worksheets were opened and rendered. The arm and creatine forms have 25 and 44 fields and passed a save/reopen check. The grip scorecard is designed for printing. The creatine chart was checked at 320 and 390 pixels, opens full-size and includes the five weekly means in the visible caption. The grip and lifting-break pages were checked at phone and desktop widths. The site index and sitemap both contain 293 distinct articles, with all four new guides in the initial 30 blog cards.

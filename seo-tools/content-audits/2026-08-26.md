## Executive read

The recent work is moving in the right direction. In the last 14 days, organic clicks rose 76%,
impressions rose 92%, average position improved by 0.73, and residual nonbrand clicks rose about
82%. GA4 organic sessions rose 44% while engagement improved 3.4 points. This is broad organic
growth rather than a brand spike.

Tools are the strongest surface. The body visualizer, physique rater, and photo body-fat estimator
produced the largest gains. The body visualizer alone rose from 27 to 738 clicks in the two-week
comparison, while its GA4 engagement rate improved from 47% to 75%. Its query market is unusually
attractive: `body visualizer` is 40,500 US monthly volume at KD 4, and the tool already averages
position 12.5 on the exact query despite having only two contextual inbound links, both from
founder posts.

The newest changes cannot yet be judged. The Aug 23 refreshes have only one day inside the Search
Console cutoff, and the Aug 25 DEXA benchmark, visual-atlas, Hume, and related changes fall after
the cutoff. The right next move is to support and instrument the body visualizer without changing
its metadata, repair the fresh DEXA benchmark's launch-contract defects, then wait for clean reads.
Do not publish another article merely to maintain cadence.

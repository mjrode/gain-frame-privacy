### Live experiments

**Body Fat From Photo result CTA** (`body_fat_result_cta_v1` / `fold_v1_stable_assignment`,
production assignments since November 11 08:47 Eastern). Registered primary measure:
share of assigned, non-forced iOS visitors with a tagged `web_download_clicked` after
assignment.

| Arm | Clickers / assigned | Rate | 24h mature | Exposed / assigned | Exposed-only rate | Keyed clicks → native arrivals |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| `analysis` (ask inside result) | 88/220 | **40.00%** | 84/202 | 219/220 | **40.18%** | 97 → 9 |
| `goal` (locked goal tile) | 42/218 | 19.27% | 40/207 | 215/218 | 19.53% | 43 → 9 |
| `sticky` (bottom bar) | 33/227 | 14.54% | 27/207 | 227/227 | 14.54% | 36 → 3 |
| `control` (bridge below result) | 26/202 | 12.87% | 25/186 | 147/202 | 17.69% | 31 → 3 |

`analysis` versus `control`: z = 6.27, p = 3.6e-10, relative lift +211%. That clears the
registered adjusted threshold of .0167 and the 20% relative-lift floor by wide margins.
`goal` (+50%, p = .076) and `sticky` (+13%, p = .62) do not clear it.

The win is not only reach. Control's bridge was recorded as seen by 147 of 202 assigned
visitors against 219 of 220 for `analysis`, so part of the gap is that the control ask
sits below the fold. Conditioning on visitors who did see a bridge, `analysis` still
converts 40.18% against control's 17.69% (z = 4.56, p = 5e-6). Placement and offer both
contribute.

Native arrivals are directional only: 9/220 assigned for `analysis` against 3/202 for
control (p = .11). Per assigned visitor `goal` matches `analysis` on arrivals (4.13% vs
4.09%) from fewer than half the clicks, so `goal` attracts fewer but better-converting
clickers. **A click lift is not an install or revenue lift**, and nothing here
establishes one.

Enrollment is 202 to 227 per arm against the registered 400. The 14-day gate completes
November 25. At the current ~17 assigned per arm per day, 400 per arm arrives about
December 5.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is the AI body fat estimate?

GainFrame uses the same visual estimation method that trained coaches use — analyzing visible muscle definition, fat distribution, and body proportions. Studies show AI photo-based estimation is within 2-4% of DEXA for most users. More importantly, the trend data from consistent weekly photos is extremely reliable — even more useful than any single DEXA reading.

Are my photos private?

Yes. Your photos are sent to AI for analysis but are never stored outside your device. No account required. No cloud storage. All data lives locally on your iPhone. Face blur and background removal are built in for sharing.

How much does GainFrame cost?

Free 1-month trial — no credit card required. After that, it's $4.99/month or $29.99/year. That's less than a single DEXA scan, and you can track your body composition every week instead of every few months.

Can I import old gym selfies?

Yes — Smart Import lets you batch-select months or years of photos from your camera roll. The AI classifies each by pose (front, back, side), extracts dates, and builds your full transformation timeline automatically. Most users start with 6-12 months of existing photos.

What makes GainFrame different from other progress photo apps?

Most progress photo apps just store and compare photos. GainFrame is an AI body composition tracker — it scores 12 individual muscle groups, estimates body fat and FFMI, generates future physique predictions, and tracks every metric over time. It replaces DEXA scans, spreadsheets, and guesswork with real data from your phone.

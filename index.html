FAQ

Common questions.

How accurate is the AI body fat estimate?

Within 2–4% of DEXA for most users — the same visual estimation method trained coaches use. The Precision Body Fat reading (from front + side + back photos together) is our highest-confidence number. The trend across consistent weekly photos is arguably more useful than any single DEXA reading.

Are my photos private?

Your photos are sent securely to Google's Gemini API for AI analysis, but they are never stored outside your device — no cloud sync, no account required. Face blur and background removal are built in for when you want to share. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

What does it cost?

Free 30-day trial — no credit card required. After that, $4.99/month or $29.99/year. That's less than a single DEXA scan, and you can measure yourself every week.

Can I import old gym selfies?

Yes. Smart Import batch-selects months or years of photos from your camera roll. The AI classifies each by pose (front, back, side), extracts the date, and builds your full transformation timeline automatically.

What makes GainFrame different from other progress photo apps?

Most apps just store and compare photos. GainFrame is a full body-composition tracker — Precision BF%, FFMI, 12 individually-scored muscle groups, Future Physique predictions, streaks, and macro targets that update per scan. It replaces the scale, the DEXA scan, and the coach-in-your-head with one app.

§ 06 FOOTNOTES

Frequently asked.

F.01How accurate is the AI body fat estimate?

GainFrame uses the same visual estimation method trained coaches use — analyzing muscle definition, fat distribution and body proportions. Studies show AI photo-based estimation is within 2–4% of DEXA for most users. The trend data from consistent weekly photos is extremely reliable — arguably more useful than any single DEXA reading.

F.02Are my photos private?

Yes. Photographs are sent to AI for analysis but never stored outside your device. No account required, no cloud storage, no syncing. Face blur and background removal are built in for sharing.

F.03How much does GainFrame cost?

Free 1-month trial, no credit card required. After that it's $4.99/month or $29.99/year — less than a single DEXA scan, and you can measure yourself every week.

F.04Can I import old gym selfies?

Yes. Smart Import batch-selects months or years of photos from your camera roll. The AI classifies each by pose, extracts dates and builds your full transformation timeline automatically.

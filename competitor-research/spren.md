## Positioning

- **Web tagline (hero):** "The Smartest Way to Measure and Improve Body Composition"
- **Web subheadline:** "Spren turns your phone into a validated health lab: Accurately measure body composition and HRV, get personalized nutrition and 24/7 AI coaching."
- **App Store name:** Spren Body Composition Scanner
- **App Store subtitle:** "Measure Body Fat Percentage"
- **Developer:** Elite HRV Inc (parent company)
- **Primary CTA (web):** "Get Started" + "Join Waitlist" (60-day free Body by Spren Plus offer for first 2,000 sign-ups)
- **Target audience signals:** Fitness enthusiasts, gym-goers, body recomposition trackers, longevity-focused, AND research/academic institutions (B2B angle).

### Positioning angle: "DEXA in your pocket"

Spren leans hard on DEXA-comparable accuracy and research validation. Direct quote from a homepage testimonial: *"I now have a DEXA scanner in my pocket."* — Mallori. Their entire positioning is: research-grade body composition, accessible via smartphone.

This is a meaningfully different positioning from GainFrame's. GainFrame says "see what your mirror can't tell you" (visual + data hybrid). Spren says "this replaces a DEXA scan" (clinical-grade alternative).

## Notes for comparison article writing

If `comparison-article-generator` ever writes "Spren vs GainFrame":

- **Verified pricing:** Free + 11 paid SKUs from $10.49/mo to $129/yr coaching. Annual range $38.99-$119.99 spans below and above GainFrame.
- **Verified accuracy claim:** "Validated by independent peer-reviewed studies, making research-grade body composition analysis accessible to anyone with a smartphone." Validation paper in **Nature** — DOI: s41430-024-01424-w.
- **Verified target audience:** Wider than GainFrame — includes serious gym-goers BUT ALSO researchers, gym-chain B2B partners, longevity-focused users, and "casual one-scan" users (via the $17.99 one-time SKU).
- **Known limitations (from missing features):**
  - **No camera roll batch import** — same gap as trackBod
  - **No per-muscle-group scoring** — they measure at the body-region level (Android/Gynoid) not muscle-group level
  - **No Future Physique prediction**
  - **No workout integration** (no Hevy, Strong, etc.)
  - **No photo timeline** — they scan, they don't archive photos as progress
  - **218.2 MB app size** — bloated, deters low-storage installs
- **Verified strengths to acknowledge:**
  - **Research validation is real and verifiable** — peer-reviewed Nature paper. GainFrame cannot match this credibility today.
  - 1,500 App Store reviews @ 4.7 — major social proof gap vs GainFrame's 20
  - Comprehensive feature set: body comp + nutrition + AI coaching + exercise planning (vs GainFrame's progress-photo-first focus)
  - Backed by parent company (Elite HRV) — not a solo-dev concern
  - 122-university institutional validation
- **Honest framing:** Spren is GainFrame's most-credentialed competitor on the body-comp axis. **Don't try to win on accuracy claims** — Spren's published Nature paper outranks GainFrame's "within 2-4% of DEXA" assertion. **GainFrame's wins are:**
  - Photo-first workflow (camera roll import, pose classification, throwback comparisons)
  - Per-muscle scoring (12 groups, progression labels) — Spren doesn't go below body-region level
  - Hevy workout integration
  - Future Physique prediction (Spren doesn't offer)
  - Lower price point ($39.99/yr vs Spren's $69.99/yr-Tier1)
  - Built for serious lifters specifically vs Spren's broader market
- **Critical:** Acknowledge Spren's research validation honestly. Hedging language: "Spren publishes a peer-reviewed validation paper, which gives them stronger institutional credibility on the accuracy claim. GainFrame's accuracy claim ('within 2-4% of DEXA for most users') is not yet independently validated."

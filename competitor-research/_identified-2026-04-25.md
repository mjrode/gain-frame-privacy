## Methodology Notes

**Phase 1 — SERP capture:** 5 parallel `WebSearch` calls (`AI body composition app`, `body fat from photo`, `body composition tracker`, `body fat percentage app`, `AI body fat estimator`). Captured top 10 domains each.

**Phase 2 — Alternative search:** 5 parallel `WebSearch` calls (`bodywhat alternatives`, `apps like bodywhat`, `best AI body composition apps 2026`, `best body fat from photo apps`, `best body fat tracking apps for lifters`).

**Phase 3 — Community signals:** 4 parallel Reddit/community searches.

**Phase 3.5 — Progress photo expansion (added mid-run per user input):** 5 parallel `WebSearch` calls (`best progress photo app iOS`, `progress photo tracking app iPhone`, `fitness progress photo app comparison`, `side by side body progress photo app`, `reddit progress pic app recommendation`).

**Phase 4 — Verification fetches:** 6 parallel `WebFetch` calls on top candidates (Progress, BodyScan, LeanLens, Coach Milo, BodyFatEstimator AI, SnapTrack). 4/6 returned full data; 2 had thin homepages requiring deeper investigation.

**Total web calls:** ~25 (acceptable parallel batch size; respectful of host limits).

**Limitations:**
- Coach Milo, abody.ai, Recomp AI, Formfy, Fitstream, LocalOne Gym Pics need URL discovery + iOS verification before full classification
- Cannot confirm without paid tools whether these competitors actually rank for our target keywords (presence in SERP top 10 is a signal but not proof of high-traffic ranking)
- App Store search not run separately — relies on web mentions of App Store URLs surfacing the iOS apps

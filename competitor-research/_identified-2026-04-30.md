## Strategic Insights

### The "AI Scanner + Workout Planner" hybrid category is forming

In April 2026, three apps (Thelo AI, Zing Coach, TrueForm AI) all launched or matured into a hybrid category that combines AI body scanning with AI workout planning. The 2026-04-25 baseline treated these as two separate categories. The new entrants have **collapsed them into one app**.

**Implication for GainFrame:**
- Our current positioning is "the AI body composition app" — purely tracking, not planning.
- These new hybrids will increasingly position themselves as "the only AI fitness app you need" because they have BOTH halves.
- GainFrame's competitive answer in content: stay focused, position as the **specialist tracking layer** (we don't write workouts, we validate them) — this is the angle the recently-published [/blog/best-ai-fitness-apps-track-body/](../blog/best-ai-fitness-apps-track-body/index.html) and [/blog/best-ai-personal-trainer-apps/](../blog/best-ai-personal-trainer-apps/index.html) already articulate.

### TrueForm AI is the most strategically interesting comparison target

TrueForm AI uses a 0-100 muscle group rating system that closely mirrors GainFrame's "Physique Score" approach. A "TrueForm AI vs GainFrame" article would likely rank well — they have a meaningful App Store footprint, the 0-100 framing is identical, and the comparison would be unusually clean.

### SKOR's GLP-1 angle is worth watching

SKOR is the first competitor we've seen explicitly target GLP-1 medication users (Ozempic/Wegovy/Mounjaro). This audience is enormous and growing. If GLP-1 becomes a content angle GainFrame wants to own (e.g. "best progress photo app for Ozempic transformation"), SKOR is the first-mover to compete with.

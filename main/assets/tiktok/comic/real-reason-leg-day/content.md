### Why the Mirror Lies to You

It’s the most common trap in the gym: you only train what you can see in the mirror.

Chest, arms, and shoulders get all the attention. Leg day? That gets skipped because "your legs are hidden in pants anyway." This is the **Mirror Problem**, and it creates a comically disproportionate physique (often called "chicken legs" or "lightbulb body").

But skipping legs doesn't just look bad—it actively harms your upper body gains.

### The Science of Proportional Growth

Your body seeks biological balance. If your leg muscles remain weak and underdeveloped, your central nervous system will act as a governor, subconsciously limiting the strength and overall growth of your upper body to prevent massive structural imbalances. 

Furthermore, heavy compound leg movements (like squats and deadlifts) trigger the highest systemic release of natural testosterone and human growth hormone (HGH). By skipping these massive multi-joint exercises, you're leaving free anabolic hormones on the table.

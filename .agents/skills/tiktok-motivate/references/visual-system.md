# TikTok Motivate Visual System

## House style: after-hours training zine

Create cinematic, documentary fitness photography with the tactile energy of a photocopied underground training zine.

- 4:5 portrait composition, designed for TikTok photo carousels.
- Realistic candid scenes, not cartoons, 3D renders, or glossy stock photography.
- Hard direct flash, dim available light, deep blacks, restrained highlights.
- Visible 35mm grain, slight motion blur, scuffed surfaces, sweat, chalk, fabric, paper, and steel texture.
- Near-monochrome charcoal palette with warm bone-white type and one restrained GainFrame-red accent (`#E53935`).
- Editorial asymmetry, generous negative space, and occasional thin rules, crop marks, or torn-paper edges.
- One photographic moment per slide. Use collages only when the concept specifically needs contrast.

This format must feel disciplined, intimate, and contemporary. Avoid military cosplay, macho parody, fake luxury, neon cyberpunk, overbuilt bodybuilders, and generic supplement-ad aesthetics.

## Typography

- Use a heavy condensed grotesk similar to Anton, Bebas Neue, or Impact.
- Use ALL CAPS for titles.
- Render titles in warm white, with at most one red word or number.
- Use a compact neutral grotesk similar to Helvetica for subtitles.
- Keep title width inside the centered 72% safe zone.
- Avoid outlined meme text, rounded pills, handwritten fonts, decorative scripts, and oversized paragraph copy.

Numbered-slide titles may include the numeral inline, such as `1. WAKE UP ON TIME`. Keep treatment consistent across a set.

Use this as scaffolding, then replace the bracketed fields:

```text

```

## Cover variation

Use a single iconic scene and 2-3 stacked title lines. Omit the subtitle unless it adds necessary context. The cover should communicate an identity change, not a list of tips.

## Habit-slide variation

Show a specific action already in progress. Favor lived-in details:

- alarm clock, sheets, shoes on the floor;
- chalked hands, headphones, worn gym flooring;
- cutting board, eggs, rice, water, meal-prep containers;
- phone facedown, unplugged controller, closed laptop;
- dog-eared book, marked page, bedside lamp.

Never show fake app screens on habit slides.

## GainFrame payoff variation

Make the visual language become slightly cleaner and more controlled while staying in the same world. Show a phone on a worn gym bench or locker-room shelf with the GainFrame app as the focal point. Use actual GainFrame screenshots and logo as references. The only required brand copy is `GAINFRAME`; feature claims must match the approved slide copy.

Do not invent a new app icon, change the GainFrame name, or add unsupported metrics. If the generated phone UI is illegible, simplify the composition and make the existing app screenshot reference larger rather than hallucinating extra controls.

## Variation levers

Change only two or three levers per new post so the format remains recognizable:

- setting: home morning, warehouse gym, hotel gym, garage gym, running track;
- film treatment: high-contrast monochrome, warm tungsten, cool fluorescent;
- graphic device: contact-sheet borders, timestamp stamps, red grease-pencil marks, torn-paper windows;
- protagonist framing: first-person hands, anonymous cropped athlete, objects-only still life;
- cadence: comeback, consistency, cut, bulk, recovery, confidence.

Keep the seven-slide arc, typography system, value-first pacing, and final GainFrame reveal stable.

# GainFrame TikTok Archive Novelty Gate

Treat the internal archive as a denylist for creative reuse.

## Scan scope

Inspect:

- `docs/assets/tiktok/**/content.md`
- `docs/assets/tiktok/**/spec.md`
- `docs/assets/tiktok/**/trend-brief.md`
- `promo-source/tik-tok-slides-jack/**`
- the active TikTok Drafts folder

Do not use finished slides from these locations as visual references.

## Fingerprint each internal post

Record:

1. Hook archetype and cover wording formula.
2. Slide sequence and reveal position.
3. Proof or payoff device.
4. Semantic promise or core content angle.
5. Product feature and interface surface used in the payoff.
6. Visual grammar and layout system.
7. CTA behavior and primary viewer job.

## Reject rules

Reject a candidate when any condition is true:

- three or more fingerprint fields match one internal post;
- the same semantic promise, product angle, or payoff is already central to a
  recent internal post, even when the new candidate uses a different palette or
  visual format;
- adapting it would require an old cover, template, mascot, type system, or
  production art direction;
- only the title, audience, colors, app lineup, or example changes;
- an old draft would occupy a slot in a requested new batch.

## Greenfield pass requirements

Every selected post must document:

- its nearest internal neighbor;
- the shared fingerprint count;
- the material difference in promise, product angle, and viewer job;
- how the researched source's native visual grammar will be preserved;
- at least three material differences from the nearest internal neighbor.

For a five-post batch, the concepts must differ in mechanism, promise, and
viewer job. They may share a broad native grammar such as raw camera-roll UGC
when that is what the current sources actually use. Never invent a metaphor or
design system merely to manufacture novelty.

Current GainFrame logos, app screenshots, and verified product facts are exempt
from the denylist because they are product truth, not finished social creative.

### The Supplement Trap

When you first start lifting, it's easy to fall into the supplement trap. You see your favorite fitness influencer chugging a neon-colored pre-workout and taking three different types of protein powder, so you assume that's the secret to getting big. 

The reality? The supplement industry is a multi-billion dollar marketing machine designed to sell you processed powder. 

### Supplements Aren't Magic

A protein shake is not a magic muscle-building potion. It is simply a highly processed, convenient source of food. If you are already getting enough protein from real food (like chicken, eggs, and beef), adding a protein shake does absolutely nothing extra for your gains. In fact, whole foods are vastly superior because they contain micronutrients, vitamins, and minerals that your body needs to properly recover and function. 

Similarly, most pre-workouts are just violently overpriced caffeine. A black coffee and a banana will give you the exact same workout energy for a fraction of the cost, without the artificial dyes or proprietary blends.

### The One Exception: Creatine

There is only one supplement that is almost universally recommended by sports scientists: **Creatine Monohydrate**. 

Creatine is one of the most heavily researched supplements in the world, with decades of proven results for increasing strength, power output, and muscle volume. It is safe, effective, and very cheap. You don't need any fancy versions—just 5 grams per day of plain creatine monohydrate.

### Save Your Money

Stop wasting hundreds of dollars a month on BCAAs, fat burners, and mass gainers. Invest that money into high-quality whole foods, prioritize your sleep, and focus on tracking your workouts.

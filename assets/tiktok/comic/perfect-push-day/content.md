### Slide 1
**Banner:** 1. START WITH BENCH PRESS  
**Copy:** Go heavy when you're fresh. This is where your chest actually grows — don't waste it.

### Slide 2
**Banner:** 2. HIT THE UPPER CHEST  
**Copy:** Most guys skip incline. That's why their upper chest is flat. Add incline DB press, full range.

### Slide 3
**Banner:** 3. PRESS FOR SHOULDERS  
**Copy:** Overhead press while you still have energy. Hit all 3 heads. Don't just do lateral raises.

### Slide 4
**Banner:** 4. FINISH WITH ISOLATION  
**Copy:** Tricep pushdowns, cable flies, lateral raises are finishers — not the main event. Save them for last.

# Topic: Your First Gym Week
**Goal:** Guide beginners through their overwhelming first week at the gym.

## Cover Slide (Slide 0)
- **Title (Black):** YOUR FIRST
- **Accent (Red):** GYM WEEK
- **Visual:** GainFrame Guy standing at the gym entrance looking overwhelmed. Lots of complicated machines and big guys lifting heavy in the background.

## Slide 1
- **Banner (White on Black, ALL CAPS):** 1. STICK TO BASICS
- **Subtext (Black, Upper/Lowercase):** Don't try 10 different machines. Pick 3-4 basic movements and master them first.
- **Visual:** GainFrame Guy looking confused at a complicated cable machine with ten handle attachments. Red 'X' over the crazy machine. Green checkmark pointing toward simple dumbbells on a rack.

## Slide 2
- **Banner:** 2. LEAVE YOUR EGO
- **Subtext:** Start with the lightest weights to learn the movement pattern. Nobody is judging you.
- **Visual:** GainFrame Guy happily lifting very small (pink/blue) dumbbells with perfect form. A huge ghost-like figure behind him lifting massive weights, but Gary isn't paying attention to it.

## Slide 3
- **Banner:** 3. HAVE A PLAN
- **Subtext:** Wandering aimlessly kills confidence. Know exactly what you are doing before walking in.
- **Visual:** GainFrame Guy confidently holding a checklist/notebook, walking directly toward a bench press.

## Slide 4
- **Banner:** 4. EMBRACE SORENESS
- **Subtext:** The soreness (DOMS) hits hardest in week 1. Drink water, eat protein, and keep moving.
- **Visual:** GainFrame Guy waking up in bed looking stiff and sore, but he has a large water bottle and a protein shake on his nightstand.

## Slide 5
- **Banner:** 5. LOG YOUR BASELINE
- **Subtext:** Don't wait until you're 'fit' to track. Download GainFrame and log Day 1. You'll thank yourself in 3 months.
- **Visual:** GainFrame Guy proudly holding up his phone displaying the GainFrame app (progress graphs/body map). He's taking a starting progress photo in the mirror.

## Timing & Strategy

| Decision | Recommendation | Why |
|----------|---------------|-----|
| **Launch Time** | **12:01 AM PST** (3:01 AM ET) | Maximizes the full 24-hour voting window |
| **Day** | **Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday** | Higher traffic = more visibility. Your competitive moat (AI body scanning with photo import) is strong enough to compete |
| **Self-Hunt vs. Hunter** | **Self-hunt** | 79% of #1 featured products are self-hunted. You know GainFrame best |
| **Pre-launch** | Set up "Coming Soon" page **now** (1+ week before launch) | Collects followers who get notified on launch day |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Your existing strategy says to wait until 10+ App Store reviews before the PH launch. Confirm you've hit that threshold before scheduling.

```
AI body scanner that turns gym selfies into data
```

**Alternates:**
- `Track your physique with AI — from your gym selfies` (52 chars)

### Description

```
GainFrame analyzes your gym selfies to estimate body fat %, BMI, FFMI,
and scores 12 individual muscle groups — no equipment needed.

Import your entire camera roll
AI classifies photos by pose (front, back, side) and builds your
transformation timeline automatically. Even years of scattered gym
selfies get organized in seconds.

Deep Dive Reports
Every photo gets a full breakdown: score ring, body composition
estimates, macro targets, and training recommendations. Compare two
photos for a detailed delta analysis showing exactly what improved.

AI Future Physique
See a predicted version of your physique at 3, 6, or 12 months —
with projected stats and an AI-generated image.

Privacy-first
No account required. Photos stay on your device. Face blur and
background removal included for safe sharing.

Built for the gym
- Ghost overlay camera for pose matching
- Apple Health weight & workout sync
- Hevy integration (exercises, sets, reps)
- Shareable analysis cards and transformation collages
- Achievement trophies for consistency streaks

Free to download. Pro unlocks unlimited AI analysis.
```

```

So I built GainFrame to solve my own problem:

2. Tap any photo — get a full body composition estimate

The "aha moment" is seeing your score jump from 48 to 78 and knowing

Some things I'm particularly proud of:

- Hevy workout integration — connect what you lifted to how you look

I've been dogfooding this for months through a beta with ~90 testers
and would love your honest feedback.

What feature would you want to see next?
```

## Launch Day Outreach Plan

### Pre-Launch (7 Days Before)

- [ ] Create "Coming Soon" page on Product Hunt
- [ ] Build supporter spreadsheet: friends, beta testers, fitness communities
- [ ] Draft individual DMs (personalized, NOT mass blasts)
- [ ] Prepare social content (threads, carousels) in advance
- [ ] Add Product Hunt "Launching Soon" badge to gainframe.app
- [ ] Email your Mailchimp list (150 subs) with a "We're launching on PH" teaser

### Launch Day Schedule

| Time (ET) | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| **3:00 AM** | Launch goes live (12:01 AM PST). Post your Maker Comment immediately |
| **7:00 AM** | Send first wave of personal DMs to closest supporters (~20 people) |
| **8:00 AM** | Post Twitter/X thread (see template below) |
| **9:00 AM** | Post LinkedIn update |
| **10:00 AM** | Send Mailchimp "Launch Day" email to full list |
| **11:00 AM** | Second DM wave (~20 more people from supporter sheet) |
| **12:00 PM** | Share in relevant Slack/Discord communities |
| **2:00 PM** | Reddit value-first posts in r/Fitness, r/GettingShredded |
| **4:00 PM** | Share progress update on Twitter ("We're #X on PH today!") |
| **6:00 PM** | Final DM wave + thank-you posts |
| **ALL DAY** | Respond to EVERY comment on PH within minutes |

> [!CAUTION]
> **NEVER say "upvote" or "vote for us."** Product Hunt's algorithm penalizes vote manipulation. Instead say:
> - "Check it out and let me know what you think"
> - "Would love your feedback"
> - "Supporting us on Product Hunt today"

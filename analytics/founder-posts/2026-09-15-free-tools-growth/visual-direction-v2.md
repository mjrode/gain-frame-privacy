# Revised visual direction

The owner requested a cleaner style after reviewing the first graphics on September 15, 2026. The new reference is a minimal white software dashboard with thin gray rules, understated charts, and small hand-drawn cartoon avatars.

## Selected direction

- Pure white backgrounds.
- Near-black sans-serif typography; regular and semibold weights.
- Fine cool-gray borders, understated grid lines, generous open space.
- A restrained clear-blue chart accent.
- Small original cartoon touches; GainFrame's scan-frame face supplies the brand identity.
- No cream, sage, forest green, gold, paper textures, foliage, serif poster typography, or dimensional effects.

The reference supplies visual treatment only. Its brand, customer data, browser chrome, and exact interface are not reused. The chart data, reporting periods, revenue definitions, and attribution limitations remain the same as in the first version.

The final five files replace the first version at the same public asset paths. The original assets are retained in git history and the image-generation output directory. The source chart renderings for the revised style are in `output/free-tools-app-growth/v2/`.

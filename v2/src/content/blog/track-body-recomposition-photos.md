<h2>How to start tracking your recomp today</h2>

<ol class="post-tips">
<li><strong>Take progress photos weekly.</strong> Same pose, same lighting. Front relaxed, front
flexed, and side are the best three angles for tracking recomp.</li>
<li><strong>Import your existing gym selfies.</strong> GainFrame can analyze photos from your camera
roll — you probably already have months of data sitting there.</li>
<li><strong>Run AI analysis monthly.</strong> Tap a photo and get body fat, FFMI, muscle group
scores, and a GainFrame Score. Track the trend, not individual readings.</li>
<li><strong>Connect Hevy.</strong> Let your workout data auto-attach to each photo so you can
correlate training volume with visual changes.</li>
<li><strong>Stop checking the scale daily.</strong> Weigh yourself if you want, but use the weekly
average for context — not as your primary metric during a recomp.</li>
</ol>

<p>Your gym selfies aren't vanity — they're the most accurate dataset you have. Stop letting a piece of
glass on your bathroom floor tell you nothing is happening.</p>

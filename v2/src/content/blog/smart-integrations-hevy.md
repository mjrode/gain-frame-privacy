<h2>Smart Album Sync: hands-free photo import</h2>

<p>Most progress photo apps require you to manually import photos every time you take one.
GainFrame's <strong>Smart Album Sync</strong> eliminates that step entirely.</p>

<p>Here's how it works:</p>

<ol class="post-steps">
<li>
<strong>Select a source album.</strong> Pick any album from your Photos library — for
example, a "Gym" album you already organize your selfies into.
</li>
<li>
<strong>GainFrame watches it.</strong> Whenever new photos appear in that album,
GainFrame automatically imports and processes them — AI classification, scoring, and
timeline insertion happen in the background.
</li>
<li>
<strong>Smart filtering.</strong> The sync engine skips screenshots, saved photos from
other apps, and non-body images. Only actual gym photos make it through.
</li>
<li>
<strong>Deletion blocklist.</strong> If you delete a photo from GainFrame, the sync
engine remembers it and won't re-import it — even if it's still in the source album.
</li>
</ol>

<h2>Why context beats photos alone</h2>

<p>A photo by itself is a data point. A photo with workout data, body composition metrics, and
weight from Apple Health is a <strong>complete snapshot</strong> of that moment in your
fitness journey.</p>

<p>When you compare two of these snapshots — say, January vs. June — you don't just see that
your shoulders got wider. You see that your overhead press volume doubled, your body fat
dropped 3%, and your weight stayed the same. <em>That's</em> useful information. It tells you
what's working and what to keep doing.</p>

<p>GainFrame is the only progress photo app that makes this connection automatically. Other apps
store photos. GainFrame contextualizes them.</p>

<h2>Setting it up</h2>

<p>Both integrations take less than a minute to enable:</p>

<ol class="post-tips">
<li><strong>Hevy:</strong> Open Settings → Integrations → Hevy. Sign in once. GainFrame
syncs your workout history and auto-attaches future workouts to matching photos.</li>
<li><strong>Album Sync:</strong> Open Settings → Album Sync → Select Album. Choose your gym
photo album. New photos import automatically from that point on.</li>
<li><strong>Apple Health:</strong> Weight data syncs automatically via HealthKit. No
additional setup needed — just allow access when prompted.</li>
</ol>

<p>Once enabled, everything runs in the background. No daily maintenance, no manual imports, no
forgetting to log things. The system works while you train.</p>

<h2>What You Can Do With GainFrame Today</h2>

<p>GainFrame turns your progress photos into actual data. You take a photo (or import the gym selfies
already on your camera roll), and the app gives you an AI-powered physique analysis — body fat
percentage, muscle group scoring, posture tracking, and a composite score that tracks your
trajectory over time.</p>

<p>The feature I'm most excited for people to try is <strong>Deep Dive Compare</strong>. Select any two
photos from your timeline and get a full side-by-side breakdown of what changed — specific muscle
groups that leveled up, body fat shifts, posture improvements, and updated macro targets for your
next training block. It takes the guesswork out of "am I actually making progress?" and replaces it
with data.</p>

<p>Beyond that, you get hands-free <strong>Guided Recording</strong> (no tripod fumbling), <strong>Ghost
Overlay</strong> for matching your previous pose perfectly, <strong>Future Physique</strong>
predictions based on your real trajectory, and automatic <strong>HealthKit</strong> integration for
weight and body fat syncing.</p>

<p>Instead of just "Chest" or "Shoulders," the AI now differentiates between:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Upper vs. Mid/Lower Chest:</strong> Essential for building that complete "shelf" look.
</li>
<li><strong>Front vs. Side/Rear Delts:</strong> Crucial for maintaining a healthy shoulder joint and
achieving a wide V-taper.</li>
<li><strong>Abs vs. Obliques:</strong> Delineating between the core muscle blocks and the connective
lines.</li>
</ul>
<p>Each sub-group is rated (e.g., Strong, Developing, Needs Work) along with a specific observation of
what the AI physically sees in the photo.</p>

<hr class="post-divider">

<h2>New: Posture &amp; Symmetry Analysis</h2>

<p>A great physique isn't just about size; it's about structural balance. Our brand new <strong>Posture
&amp; Symmetry</strong> section acts as a virtual physical assessment.</p>

<div class="post-hero-image scroll-reveal" style="margin-top: 2rem; margin-bottom: 2rem;">
<div class="phone-frame phone-frame-blog">

</div>

<p>The AI now identifies subtle alignment issues that can lead to injury or aesthetic imbalances. For
example, it can spot:</p>
<ul>
<li>Shoulder levelness across the clavicles.</li>
<li>Abdominal midline shifting or structural lean.</li>
<li>Internal rotation of the shoulders (very common for heavy bench pressers), prompting
recommendations for rear delt mobility.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Goal-Driven Action Plans &amp; Macros</h2>

<p>Data without direction is useless. The updated Deep Dive now translates all of these visual
observations into an incredibly specific action plan.</p>

<div class="post-hero-image scroll-reveal" style="margin-top: 2rem; margin-bottom: 2rem;">
<div class="phone-frame phone-frame-blog">
<img src="/blog/how-ai-body-composition-analysis-works/assets/recs.webp"
alt="GainFrame Deep Dive Recommendations showing highly specific exercises targeting visual weak points">
</div>
</div>

<p>The <strong>Recommendations</strong> section takes your lagging body parts and gives you exact
exercises to fix them. If your upper chest is rated as "Developing," the AI won't just say "train
chest more." It will tell you to <em>"start your chest days with Incline Dumbbell Presses (30-45
degree angle) to target the Upper Chest."</em></p>

<p>Finally, we've introduced <strong>Estimated Daily Targets</strong> and a <strong>Progress
Trajectory</strong>.</p>

<div class="post-hero-image scroll-reveal" style="margin-top: 2rem; margin-bottom: 2rem;">
<div class="phone-frame phone-frame-blog">

<p>By combining the AI's visual body fat estimate with your height, weight, and chosen fitness goal
(Lose Weight, Gain Muscle, or Body Recomp), GainFrame now calculates highly accurate daily macro
targets (Calories, Protein, Carbs, Fat). The Progress Trajectory then forecasts what you can expect
over the next 12-16 weeks if you stick to the plan.</p>

<h2>Comparing photos with workout context</h2>

<p>The real magic of the Hevy integration happens when you compare two photos months apart.</p>

<p>Instead of just eyeballing whether your shoulders look wider, GainFrame's AI comparison tells you
exactly how much your shoulder muscles improved visually (e.g., "Developing → Strong"). And
right
below that result, you can see your Hevy logs.</p>

<p>You might notice that your lateral raise volume doubled over those six months. Or that you
switched
from dumbbell to barbell overhead press. You're no longer guessing about what works — you have
the
workout data and the visual proof sitting side-by-side.</p>

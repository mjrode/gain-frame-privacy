# Screen Recording Plan + Google Vids Guide

Your goal: Record key app moments on your iPhone → Upload to Google Vids (vids.new) → Get a polished 9:16 vertical video for TikTok and Instagram Reels.

---

## Part 1: What to Record (Shot List)

Record each of these as a **separate clip** on your iPhone (screen recording via Control Center). Keep each clip **5-10 seconds** — Google Vids will stitch and trim them. Use the app with real data so it looks authentic, not staged.

> [!TIP]
> **Before recording**: Turn on Do Not Disturb, hide the notch/Dynamic Island if possible, and make sure your battery isn't at a distracting percentage. Record in portrait (9:16) since that's the final format.

### Shot 1: The Opening Hook (App Launch → Home Dashboard)
- Open GainFrame fresh
- Show the home screen with real progress data visible
- **Why**: Establishes what the app looks like — first impression
- **Duration**: 3-4 seconds

### Shot 2: Daily Check-In Camera Flow
- Tap to start a daily check-in
- Show the camera opening with the pose guide overlay
- Show the framing quality indicator turning green as you position yourself
- **Why**: This is the core daily action — shows ease of use
- **Duration**: 5-8 seconds

### Shot 3: Ghost Overlay Alignment
- Show a check-in photo with the ghost overlay of a previous photo
- Adjust/align if the UI allows it
- **Why**: This is a unique, visually striking feature — great for "wait, what is that?" reactions
- **Duration**: 4-6 seconds

### Shot 4: AI Analysis Results Appearing
- Show a photo being analyzed → score appearing
- Show the body fat %, muscle score, and feedback text loading in
- **Why**: The "magic moment" — AI giving you real data from a photo
- **Duration**: 5-7 seconds

### Shot 5: ⭐ Precision BF% (Hero Feature)
- Show the "Precision BF% Available — 2 poses ready to analyze" card appearing
- Tap it → show the analysis running
- Show the result: "✦ 18%" with the confidence badge (Med/High)
- Tap the info icon → show the Confidence Levels popup
- **Why**: This is your differentiator. The study data makes this credible. Build the whole video around this moment.
- **Duration**: 8-10 seconds (longest clip — this is the payoff)

### Shot 6: Precision BF% Info Sheet
- Show the "How It Works" info sheet (Multiple Angles, Health Data Boost, Confidence Score)
- Scroll to "For Best Results" showing front + side pose illustrations
- **Why**: Educational — shows the science behind it
- **Duration**: 5-7 seconds

### Shot 7: Deep Dive Report
- Open a Deep Dive analysis on a photo
- Scroll through the detailed breakdown (muscle groups, symmetry, recommendations)
- **Why**: Shows depth — this isn't a toy, it's a real analysis tool
- **Duration**: 5-7 seconds

### Shot 8: Trend Dashboard / Progress Over Time
- Navigate to the trends view
- Show body fat trending down or score trending up over weeks/months
- **Why**: Proves the app is useful long-term, not just a one-time gimmick
- **Duration**: 4-6 seconds

### Shot 9: Transformation / Then vs Now
- Show a before/after comparison (if you have enough data)
- The "Personal Record" transformation pair if available
- **Why**: The emotional payoff — visible progress
- **Duration**: 4-5 seconds

### Shot 10: "Read the Research" Link
- Tap "Read the research →" from the Precision BF% info sheet
- Show the blog post loading briefly
- **Why**: Credibility — shows you're backed by actual science, not just claiming accuracy
- **Duration**: 2-3 seconds

## Part 3: Google Vids Tips

### How to Use vids.new

1. Go to [vids.new](https://vids.new) in Chrome
2. Select **"Ingredients to Video"**
3. Upload your screen recording clips (the raw .MOV files from your iPhone)
4. Paste one of the prompts above as your description
5. Set aspect ratio to **9:16 (vertical)**
6. Generate → Review → Edit in the timeline if needed

### Prompt Tips for Better Output

- **Be specific about what you DON'T want**: "No stock footage, no generic gym b-roll, no cheesy transitions"
- **Specify text style**: "Bold white sans-serif text on dark semi-transparent overlays"
- **Name the vibe**: "Clean and modern like an Apple product demo" works better than "professional"
- **Music direction**: "Upbeat lo-fi electronic" or "trending TikTok audio feel" — avoid just saying "energetic"
- **Mention pacing**: "Let each screen recording clip play for at least 3 seconds before cutting"

### Post-Edit Checklist

- [ ] Does the video look good with sound OFF? (Most people scroll muted)
- [ ] Are text overlays readable on a phone screen? (Not too small)
- [ ] Is the pacing fast enough to hold attention past 3 seconds?
- [ ] Does it end with a clear CTA? (App name + "link in bio")
- [ ] Is the total length under 60s for TikTok, under 90s for Reels?

## Part 4: Recording Tips

- **iPhone screen recording**: Swipe down from top-right → tap the record button → 3-second countdown → use the app → swipe down again to stop
- **Hide sensitive data**: Use sample data or your own real data if you're comfortable
- **Record multiple takes**: Better to have 3 takes of each shot and pick the cleanest one
- **Steady scrolling**: Scroll smoothly and slowly — jerky movements look amateur in recordings
- **Pause on key moments**: When the Precision BF% result appears, **pause for 2 full seconds** so Google Vids can grab that frame

## User Interview Notes

**Q: What is the single most frustrating thing about using a smart scale to measure body fat?**
- Smart scales lie with confidence, giving an "illusion of precision".
- It's a "best guess" based on electrical resistance (hydration levels). 
- Hydrated = thinks you're lean. Dehydrated = thinks you're fat.
- Causes a frustrating loop for people training hard, resulting in weird 2% daily jumps that drive you crazy.

**Why AI Photo Analysis is Better:**
- Looks at geometry and definition, not electrical resistance.
- It's a digital version of the "mirror test", backed by data from thousands of DEXA scans.
- Extremely useful for runners and lifters because it recognizes muscle separation and vascularity (which a scale is blind to).
- Provides a steadier, mathematically sound trend line.

Three quick progress photo tips from GainFrame Guy

Since you're here, here's the actual substance — three tips that will immediately improve your progress tracking:

  1. Same time, every time. Take photos first thing in the morning, before eating or drinking. Your body weight and water retention fluctuate throughout the day by 2–4 lbs. Comparing a morning photo to an evening photo isn't useful data.
  2. Same pose, same spot. Pick a spot in your home with consistent natural light. Stand the same distance from the camera, same angle. Even minor pose changes can make muscle look like it appeared or disappeared overnight.
  3. Weekly beats forever. You don't need to track daily. Weekly is enough to catch the trend. If you miss a week, don't try to compensate — just pick up where you left off. Consistency over a long window is what matters.

These three things cost you nothing and immediately make your progress photos 10x more useful. No app required — though if you want to go further, GainFrame layers AI body composition on top of the photos you're already taking.

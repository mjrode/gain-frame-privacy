The U.S. Navy tape-measure method used in our body fat calculator has been validated to estimate body fat within ±3–4% of more expensive methods like DEXA. It is one of the most reliable self-assessment tools available. For even greater accuracy, GainFrame's AI-powered photo analysis can provide more detailed body composition estimates.

TDEE stands for Total Daily Energy Expenditure — the total number of calories you burn per day including exercise and daily activity. It matters because knowing your TDEE is the foundation of any diet plan: eat below it to lose fat, at it to maintain, or above it to build muscle.

Set your goal to "lean bulk" which adds roughly 300 calories above your TDEE. The calculator will prioritize protein (around 0.8 g per pound of body weight), set fat at 25% of calories, and fill the rest with carbohydrates. Combine these macros with progressive resistance training for optimal results.

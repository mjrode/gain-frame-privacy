Yes — every calculator here is 100% free. No account, no email, no signup. Enter your measurements, get your result. If you want AI-powered photo analysis and tracking beyond the math, the GainFrame iOS app extends the set.

The U.S. Navy tape-measure method used here has been validated to within ±3–4% of DEXA for most people — one of the most reliable self-assessment methods available. For higher precision, GainFrame's AI photo analysis triangulates a more exact number from a single image.

TDEE — Total Daily Energy Expenditure — is the calories you burn per day, resting plus moving. It's the foundation of every diet plan: eat under it to lose fat, at it to maintain, above it to build muscle. Miss the number, miss the outcome.

Set the goal to lean bulk — roughly 300 calories above TDEE. The calculator prioritizes protein (~0.8g per pound of bodyweight), caps fat at ~25% of calories, and fills the rest with carbs. Pair with progressive resistance training.

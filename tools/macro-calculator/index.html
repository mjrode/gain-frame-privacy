How This Works

This calculator first estimates your TDEE using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, adjusts it for your goal, then splits calories into macros using evidence-based guidelines:

  • Protein: 1.0 g/lb (cutting) · 0.8 g/lb (maintaining/bulking)
  • Fat: 25% of total calories
  • Carbs: Remaining calories after protein & fat

Tips for Success

  • Hit your protein target first — it's the most important macro for body composition
  • Don't stress about hitting carbs/fat exactly — ±10 g is fine
  • Track for 2–3 weeks, then adjust if your weight isn't moving as expected
  • These are starting points, not gospel — your body will tell you what it needs

How It's Calculated

Step 1 · Total calories to change ΔWeight × 3,500 cal per lb
Step 2 · Daily deficit or surplus Weekly rate × 3,500 ÷ 7
Step 3 · Daily calorie target TDEE ± Daily deficit/surplus

The 3,500 calories per pound (7,700 per kilogram) rule is a useful approximation, not an exact law. Real fat loss is slightly slower over time because your TDEE drops as you lose weight — expect the actual timeline to run 5–15% longer than predicted. Re-run the math every 2–4 weeks based on how the scale actually moves.

Protein matters. In a deficit, eating 0.8–1 g of protein per pound of bodyweight preserves muscle mass. In a surplus, the same protein target maximizes lean gain over fat gain.

- **Recommended skill:** `blog-post-generator` (how-to)

- **Why opportunity:** Emerging behavior — millions of gym-goers are now using ChatGPT to write their routines.   - Why ChatGPT plans drift (no progressive overload, no recovery awareness, no body comp feedback)
  - The 3-week validation rule (track strength + body comp before iterating)
  - Tools to track the result (tape, scale, GainFrame for body comp)

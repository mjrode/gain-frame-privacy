# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Serious gym-goers, especially intermediate lifters aged 18–35, who already track training and want a credible visual record of body-composition progress. On public community surfaces, visitors need to understand rank, score history, and deliberately shared profile details quickly without the experience turning bodies into popularity content.

## Product Purpose

GainFrame turns progress photos into an organized, privacy-conscious record of physique change. The public leaderboard adds opt-in accountability and recognition while keeping the member in control of what appears. Success means progress feels legible, motivating, and trustworthy without compromising consent or dignity.

## Brand Personality

Gym-native, candid, and encouraging. The interface should feel precise enough to trust, playful enough to remember, and confident without becoming clinical or macho. GainFrame Guy supplies warmth and identity without competing with the user’s data.

## Anti-references

- Generic fitness dashboards built from interchangeable metric cards.
- Aggressive bodybuilding aesthetics, neon-on-black gym branding, or gamification that judges bodies.
- Clinical health interfaces that overstate the precision of photo-based estimates.
- Social feeds that reward reactions, popularity, or involuntary sharing.
- Decorative effects that reduce readability or obscure the mascot and other meaningful content.

## Design Principles

1. Make progress understandable at a glance, then reveal supporting history.
2. Treat privacy and opt-in sharing as visible product behavior, not fine print.
3. Use the mascot as an encouraging guide and recognizable signature, never as background noise.
4. Keep data presentation calm and credible while preserving a gym-native voice.
5. Prefer a few strong moments of personality over decoration on every surface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and interaction patterns. Preserve keyboard focus, semantic controls, 44px touch targets where practical, reduced-motion behavior, zoom-safe text, and responsive layouts down to 320px. Never rely on color alone to communicate rank, progress, privacy, or status.

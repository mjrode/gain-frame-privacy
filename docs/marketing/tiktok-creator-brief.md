## 1. What the app is

**GainFrame: Gym Progress Photos** — an AI body composition app for serious gym-goers. Take or import a gym photo and the AI returns a body fat estimate, a 1–100 physique score, FFMI, and scores for 12 individual muscle groups. It then tracks all of that over time with before/after comparisons.

- Tagline: *See your gains, frame by frame.* / *See what your mirror can't tell you.*
- iOS only (no Android planned — solo developer)
- App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gainframe/id6759252082
- Website: https://gainframe.app
- Rating: **4.9 stars** (34 ratings), launched March 11, 2026
- TikTok: **@gainframeapp** · Instagram: @gainframeapp

Built by a solo founder (me — Michael, software engineer, 20 years lifting). "Built by a lifter, not a fitness brand" is on-brand framing.

## 2. Target audience

**Primary (aim ~80% of content here):**
- Serious gym-goers, 18–35, intermediate lifters, natural bodybuilders, recomp-focused
- Already track workouts in Hevy/Strong/JEFIT but have no visual progress tracking
- Take gym mirror selfies regularly, never organize or compare them

**Secondary:** anyone on a recomp / muscle gain / weight loss journey who wants proof of progress beyond the scale — including people with years of gym photos sitting in their camera roll.

**Not the audience:** casual "get fit" users, generic weight-loss crowds. The voice is gym-native (recomp, cut/bulk, FFMI, macros) — never "tone up" / "burn fat" language.

## 3. Features + what hooks people

Full feature list: AI Deep Dive report (score, BF%, FFMI, 12 muscle scores, macros), Deep Dive Compare, Future Physique, Smart Import, precision compare with auto-alignment, Throwback cards, Ghost Overlay camera, face blur + background removal, Apple Health + Hevy integrations.

**The five things no competitor has — these are the TikTok hooks:**

1. **Camera roll import with AI pose sorting.** Import hundreds of old gym pics; AI classifies by pose and skips non-body photos. Instant transformation timeline from photos you already have. (Strong "I found photos from 2 years ago" content angle.)
2. **Future Physique.** Pick 3/6/12 months, AI generates a projected image of your future body with predicted stats. Nobody else has this — it's the most visually stop-scrolling feature.
3. **Body composition from any mirror selfie — no tripod, no floor markers.** Competitors literally require setup; GainFrame auto-aligns.
4. **12 muscle-group scores** with progression labels (Needs Work → Developing → Strong), before/after muscle heat-map. Very visual.
5. **Hevy integration** — the day's workout volume attaches to the day's photo.

What retains users (from our data, hypothesis not proven): the weekly check-in streak loop and the AI Coach chat that answers using the user's real numbers.

## 9. Guardrails

- **No hype language** ("game-changing", "revolutionary") and no motivation-quote content.
- **AI accuracy claims must be hedged:** it's an estimate, "within roughly 2–4% of DEXA for most users," never "medically accurate." Always fine to show the in-app disclaimer.
- **Privacy claims must be exact:** photos are analyzed by AI but never stored on any server; everything lives on-device, no account required. (Photos do leave the device briefly during analysis — don't claim "100% on-device.")
- App is **iOS only** — don't tease Android.
- The user is the hero, the AI is the instrument: "you did the work, the app reveals the data."

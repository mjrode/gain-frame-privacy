## What I'd do next

1. **Keep paid closed and dissect the 7/5 trial cohort before 7/26.** Compare its acquisition mix, hard-paywall assignment, onboarding completion, and first-week product use with the three 43–58% cohorts. The immediate question is whether 23.1% is traffic-mix dilution, an experiment split, or a real conversion regression.

Add a short cancel-reason capture on the in-app cancellation path or a webhook-triggered churn email, then choose the save-play experiment from the evidence (yearly framing, pause, or win-back). Do not redirect the roadmap toward generic retention features; W1/W4 usage retention remains healthy.
3. **Double down on the SEO surfaces already converting attention into clicks.** Expand the body-fat photo/tool cluster and the founder-playbook cluster, improve snippets on high-impression/low-CTR pages, and finish campaign-token coverage across every App Store CTA (55.2% of clickers are tagged today; target greater than 90%).

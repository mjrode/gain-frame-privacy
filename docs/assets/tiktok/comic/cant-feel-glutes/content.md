# Can't Feel Your Glutes? 🍑

If you’re doing squats and deadlifts but only feeling your quads and lower back, you’re missing out on the biggest muscle in your body.

Here is the exact framework to fix your mind-muscle connection and start building your glutes.

**1. IT'S A QUAD SQUAT**
Most people squat straight up and down, keeping their torso vertical. This shifts all the tension to your quads (front of your legs). To hit your glutes, you need to initiate the movement by pushing your hips back. 

**2. THE HIP HINGE**
To load the glutes, you must hinge. Push your hips straight back. Think of closing a car door with your butt. Your shins should remain mostly vertical. If your knees are shooting forward, you're using your quads.

**3. BULGARIAN SPLIT SQUATS**
The undisputed king of glute isolation. Elevate your rear foot. Lean your torso forward slightly over your front working leg. Push through your heel. If you stand straight up, you'll feel it in your quad. Lean forward. 

**4. MIND-MUSCLE CONNECTION**
Don't just move the weight from point A to point B. At the top of every rep (especially hip thrusts and bridges), physically squeeze your glutes hard. Force the muscle to contract under the load.

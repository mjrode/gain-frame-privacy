## Shared identity lock

Preserve the same facial structure, dark brown wavy hair and hairline, eyebrows, hazel-green eyes, nose, ears, genuine smile and teeth, light olive skin tone, trimmed mustache and short beard pattern, and apparent age. Do not beautify or idealize into a different person. Keep every composition square and entirely safe inside X's circular avatar crop. No text, logos, watermarks, glasses, hats, or random accessories unless the individual concept explicitly calls for a prop.

## When is a paid progress-photo app useful?

Pay when a specific feature solves a recurring problem: automatic imports, a larger check-in allowance, a reliable export workflow or deeper analysis. You can [compare paid progress photo apps](/blog/best-progress-photo-apps/) against those jobs.

Before choosing, check these five points:

- Can you import two old photos with the correct dates?
- Does your needed comparison mode work without payment?
- Can you retrieve the original photos later?
- Does an export add a watermark or require a purchase?
- Is the usage limit clear before you build your archive?

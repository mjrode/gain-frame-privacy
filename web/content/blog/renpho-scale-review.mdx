## What do you actually get for $30?

Renpho sells a family of Bluetooth and Wi-Fi smart scales, most commonly in the $20–35 range, that measure weight and estimate body composition — body fat, muscle mass, water, and a dozen-plus related metrics — via foot-to-foot bioelectrical impedance (BIA). A small current runs up one leg and down the other, and an algorithm models your whole body from that lower-body sample.

Readings sync automatically to the RENPHO Health app, which holds a strong rating on the App Store — around 4.7 across roughly 7,200 ratings at the time of writing — and connects onward to Apple Health and similar platforms. That app quality is a real part of the value: plenty of budget scales ship with software that feels like an afterthought, and Renpho's commonly gets praised as the reason people stick with the habit.

The pitch, in short: full body composition tracking for the price of a single InBody scan at a gym. Whether that pitch holds depends entirely on which number you're buying it for.

## What does the Renpho scale do well?

-   **Price-to-function is close to unbeatable.** Around $30 for accurate weighing, automatic sync, trend charts, and multi-user support. Nothing else in body tracking delivers this much workflow per dollar.
-   **The weight measurement is the quiet star.** Renpho scales are commonly reported to weigh accurately and consistently — and weight is the input every serious tracking method actually needs. An automated, zero-effort weight trend is most of what a scale should be.
-   **The sync removes the failure point.** Manual weight logging dies in week three. Step on, walk away, the chart updates. Habit-proofing the measurement is arguably worth the $30 by itself.
-   **The app clears the budget-scale bar easily.** Clean trends, data export, broad platform sync, solid rating. Reviewer opinions vary on the deeper features, but the core loop is commonly described as frictionless.

## Is the Renpho body fat number accurate?

This is the honest center of the review: the body fat percentage is an estimate, and a volatile one.

Foot-to-foot BIA measures the electrical resistance of your lower body, then models the rest. Resistance depends heavily on water, so the estimate moves with hydration, meal timing, alcohol, training, and time of day — commonly by 2–4 points within a normal week in which your actual body fat barely changed. The number also carries a model bias: the algorithm guesses your torso and upper body rather than measuring them. We've covered the mechanics in depth in our [smart scale accuracy breakdown](/blog/are-smart-scales-accurate/), and Renpho behaves exactly like the category.

The practical reading: **fine for long-term trends, unreliable for absolutes.** Weigh in under consistent conditions — same time of day, before food — and the multi-week direction of the body fat estimate carries some signal. Any single morning's number, and especially any day-to-day comparison, is mostly noise. If a 25.3% reading after taco night can ruin your morning, the kindest setting is to hide the metric and keep the weight trend.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the Renpho scale accurate?

Split the question in two. For body weight, yes — Renpho scales are commonly reported to track weight reliably, which is the metric the whole product hinges on. For body fat and the other composition metrics, no single reading should be trusted: they're foot-to-foot BIA estimates that can swing several points with hydration, meal timing, and time of day.

### Why does my Renpho body fat percentage change every day?

Because the scale measures electrical resistance through your lower body, and your hydration changes daily. A salty dinner, a hard workout, alcohol, or weighing at a different time shifts your body water enough to move the estimate 2–4 points. Real body fat changes by tenths of a percent per week — daily swings are measurement noise, not fat.

### Is a Renpho scale worth it in 2026?

For automated weight tracking, absolutely — around $30 buys accurate weigh-ins, automatic sync, a well-rated app, and a trend chart, which is most of what a daily scale habit needs. Buy it for the weight trend and treat the composition numbers as a loose bonus. If the body fat number is your main reason for buying, look elsewhere.

### Does Renpho require a subscription?

The core experience has not required one — you buy the scale and the app's weigh-in sync, trends, and composition metrics work without a paid plan. Renpho has offered premium app features at various points and offerings change, so check the current listing if a strictly subscription-free experience matters to you.

### What is better than a Renpho scale for body composition?

Depends on budget and what you want fixed. Multi-point BIA devices like the Hume Body Pod or Withings Body Scan add hand electrodes for a better estimate. Photo-based analysis tracks visible shape and sidesteps hydration noise entirely. A periodic DEXA scan is the accuracy anchor.

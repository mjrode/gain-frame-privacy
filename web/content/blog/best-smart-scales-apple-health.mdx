## 1\. [Withings Body Smart](https://www.withings.com/us/en/body-smart): Best Apple Health sync, full stop

**App:** [Withings on the iOS App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id542701020), rated about 4.6 stars across roughly 360,000 ratings at the time of writing · **Price:** commonly listed around $100 to 130

Withings has been the HealthKit reference scale for a decade, and the Body Smart shows why. Wi-Fi sync means the reading uploads the moment you step off; your phone can be in another room, off, or at work. Weight, body fat, lean mass, and a stack of secondary metrics land in Apple Health with no ritual attached.

The companion app is the most polished in the category, handles multiple household users automatically, and Apple sells Withings scales in its own store, which says something about the integration's reliability. The BIA composition numbers carry the same hydration caveats as every scale here.

<div className="post-render-figure scroll-reveal"><img src="assets/withings-app.webp" alt="The Withings app measurements screen showing sleep quality, ECG, and health readings next to a Withings scale and watch" loading="lazy" /><p className="post-caption">The Withings app is a full health dashboard, and every measurement it collects can flow into Apple Health.</p></div>

**Best for:** anyone who wants the sync to be invisible. **Limitations:** triple the price of a budget scale for the same weight number; the wider Withings ecosystem is easy to overbuy into. The step-up [Body Scan model](/blog/withings-body-scan-review/) adds segmental analysis at a much steeper price.

## 2\. [Wyze Scale X](https://www.wyze.com/products/wyze-scale-x): Best budget pick

**App:** [Wyze on the iOS App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1288415553), rated about 4.6 stars across roughly 440,000 ratings at the time of writing · **Price:** commonly listed around $35 to 50

The Scale X is the value anomaly in this list: 13 body metrics, automatic recognition for up to 8 users, and clean Apple Health sync at a price close to a dumb scale. Mainstream testing outlets have repeatedly made it a top overall pick, and its combination of price and feature count is the reason.

The tradeoff is Bluetooth-only sync, so readings reach Apple Health when the Wyze app can talk to the scale, and a companion app that is mostly a smart-home hub. You will scroll past camera feeds to reach your weight chart. The sync itself, once configured, is dependable.

**Best for:** the most metrics per dollar with working Apple Health sync. **Limitations:** phone-dependent Bluetooth sync; the app serves cameras and vacuums first, scales second.

## 3\. [Eufy Smart Scale P2 Pro](https://www.eufy.com/): Best mid-range option

**App:** [eufy Life on the iOS App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1153481724), rated about 4.8 stars across roughly 17,000 ratings at the time of writing · **Price:** commonly listed around $70 to 80

The P2 Pro sits in the gap between the budget tier and Withings: Wi-Fi sync like the expensive scales, around 16 measurements, water resistance, and a dedicated fitness app rather than a smart-home hub. Wirecutter has recommended it as a strong alternative pick, and its eufy Life app carries one of the best ratings of any scale companion app.

Wi-Fi matters here for the same reason it does at the top of the list: the sync happens whether or not your phone is nearby, which quietly removes the most common way budget-scale data goes missing.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best smart scale for Apple Health?

The Withings Body Smart is the most reliable pick: Wi-Fi sync means readings land in Apple Health without opening an app, and Withings has maintained HealthKit support longer than any other scale brand. If you want to spend less, the Wyze Scale X and Eufy P2 Pro sync well at roughly a third of the price.

### Can you connect any smart scale to Apple Health?

Most current smart scales can, through their companion app. You enable Apple Health sharing inside the scale's app, approve the permissions, and readings flow into Health from then on. No scale talks to Apple Health directly; the companion app is always the bridge, which is why its quality matters as much as the hardware.

### What data do smart scales write to Apple Health?

Weight always, and on most models body fat percentage, lean body mass, and BMI. Some brands also write body water or heart rate where the hardware supports it. Which metrics arrive depends on both the scale and its app permissions, so it is worth checking the Health app's data sources after the first sync.

### Do smart scales sync to Apple Health automatically?

Wi-Fi scales do: step on, walk away, and the reading uploads on its own. Bluetooth-only scales, including most RENPHO and budget models, generally need the companion app open or recently active on a nearby phone to receive the reading before passing it to Apple Health. That difference is the main thing extra money buys.

### Are the body fat numbers from these scales accurate?

Treat them as rough estimates. Every scale here uses bioelectrical impedance, which commonly disagrees with DEXA by several percentage points and swings with hydration day to day. The weight number is measured directly and is trustworthy. The composition numbers are most useful as a long-run trend rather than absolute values.

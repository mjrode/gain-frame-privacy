## Which app tracks physique changes over time?

### 1. GainFrame: scored progress photos

[Official site](https://gainframe.app) · [US App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gainframe-progress-photos/id6759252082) · **4.89/5 from 62 ratings** (September 21, 2026).

<div className="post-render-figure"><img src="assets/gainframe-app.webp" alt="A real comparison screen pairs dated progress photos with estimated physique metrics." loading="lazy" /><p className="post-caption">App screenshot from our library. A real comparison screen pairs dated progress photos with estimated physique metrics.</p></div>

The photo workflow supports imports, guided poses and comparisons between dated check-ins. Photo-based physique estimates add another layer to the record. Advanced reports and AI Compare are Pro features, and selected photos are processed by an AI service when you request analysis.

For a physique tracker, check five things before you commit:

- **Existing-photo import:** bring your baseline into the same history.
- **Pose matching:** find front-to-front or side-to-side comparisons.
- **Comparison history:** inspect more than the newest check-in.
- **Muscle detail:** understand which of the 12 muscle-group estimates have a usable view.
- **Workout context:** connect Hevy if you want training beside visual changes.

Automatic scoring is available within free capture/import limits. Newer and legacy installations can have different allowances, so use the limit shown in your app. Unlimited imports and deeper analysis require Pro.

**Choose it for:** an ongoing visual record with optional analysis. **Limit:** iOS only; the outputs are estimates from photos. You can [try a physique assessment](/tools/physique-rater/) in the browser or [compare two check-in photos](/tools/progress-photo-compare/) before choosing a tracker. Our [progress photo app comparison](/blog/best-progress-photo-apps/) covers the capture and export differences.

### 2. Apple Photos: a basic photo archive

[Official site](https://www.apple.com/ios/photos/) · [US App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/photos/id1584215428) · **3.07/5 from 15,650 ratings** (September 21, 2026).

<div className="post-render-figure"><img src="assets/photos-app.webp" alt="Apple Photos provides a general photo library and collections." loading="lazy" /><p className="post-caption">Developer-supplied App Store screenshot. Apple Photos provides a general photo library and collections.</p></div>

An album gives you a dated archive without another fitness subscription. Use consistent front, side and back poses and retain the original files. The locked Hidden album can keep the images out of the main photo library.

**Choose it for:** a simple habit with minimal setup. **Limit:** no dedicated physique scoring or guided side-by-side workflow; storage follows your device and iCloud allowance. A [repeatable photo schedule](/blog/how-often-progress-photos/) still matters when your archive is simple.

### 3. Spren: structured composition check-ins

[Official site](https://www.spren.com) · [US App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/spren-body-composition-scanner/id1664503999) · **4.74/5 from 1,490 ratings** (September 21, 2026).

Spren markets photo-based body-composition reports with body-fat and lean-mass estimates. Its listing also describes nutrition planning and subscription access. Follow the capture instructions and keep scan conditions repeatable.

**Choose it for:** periodic scan-based estimates. **Limit:** vendor accuracy claims are not independent validation of your result. This review did not verify a durable free scan allowance. The [Spren review](/blog/spren-app-review/) covers the product in more detail.

### 4. Recomp AI: 3D scan comparisons

[Official site](https://isolabsllc.com) · [US App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/recomp-ai-3d-body-scanner/id6752960004) · **4.65/5 from 748 ratings** (September 21, 2026).

The app from ISO Labs advertises body-fat, lean-mass and circumference estimates with side-by-side 3D model comparisons. The current listing makes advanced analysis and premium features subscription-only.

**Choose it for:** tracking a series of scan-based body models during a recomp. **Limit:** the estimates depend on the scan and should not be treated as exact changes in muscle tissue. See the [Recomp AI comparison](/blog/recomp-ai-vs-gainframe/) for the difference between scans and photos.

[Official site](https://www.hevyapp.com) · [US App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hevy-workout-tracker-gym-log/id1458862350) · **4.92/5 from 93,597 ratings** (September 21, 2026).

[Official site](https://macrofactorapp.com/macrofactor/) · [US App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/macrofactor-macro-tracker/id1553503471) · **4.84/5 from 22,390 ratings** (September 21, 2026).

## How do you choose a manageable combination?

| Your main question | Start here | Add if useful |
|---|---|---|
| How is my physique changing? | Repeatable progress photos | Training record |
| How are my measurements changing? | Tape or scan-based measurement record | Matching photos |
| What is my weight trend? | Consistent scale record | Food intake log |
| Am I progressing in training? | Workout log | Dated photo comparison |

First, choose the unanswered question. Second, try a small record in the relevant category. Third, add a second tracker only if it gives you useful context you will actually review.

## What else should you know?

### What is the best body tracking app?

<p>Choose according to the information you need. Photo trackers document visible physique changes, scan apps estimate body composition or circumferences, and connected scale apps record weight trends. Workout and nutrition logs add context about your habits. Test a small record first so you know whether the category answers your question.</p>

### Which app works as a physique tracker?

<p>A physique tracker should keep dated photos together, help you repeat poses and let you compare matching views over time. Some also add muscle-group estimates and training context. Check existing-photo imports, the comparison workflow and current usage limits before committing your full history to a particular app.</p>

### Do I need more than one body tracking app?

<p>You may find a second app useful when it answers a different question, such as pairing physique photos with your training log. Start with one workflow you can maintain. Add another only when you know what missing context it supplies and how you will use that information.</p>

### Are body tracking app measurements exact?

<p>That depends on the input and method, but photo and scan-based body-composition outputs are estimates. Camera conditions, pose and the model can affect them. Connected devices also have their own measurement methods. Review comparable records over time and avoid interpreting one small change as a precise change in muscle or fat.</p>

### Is there a useful free body tracking option?

<p>A photo album and a repeatable capture routine can create a useful free visual record within your available storage. Some dedicated apps also provide limited free check-ins or workout logging. Confirm the feature allowance you need, since free downloads may still charge for imports, history, comparisons or advanced reports.</p>

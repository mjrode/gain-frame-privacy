## Which is better for you, InBody H30 or H40?

**H30 is the value choice** when you want a recurring weight and composition record in the InBody app. It already provides the hand-and-foot measurement format and connected workflow that may be your main reasons for considering this brand.

**H40 is the more relevant choice** if you specifically want its body-balance evaluation and leg-muscle output. Decide in advance how you would review that information alongside training. A regional label alone should not dictate your next workout.

If neither workflow feels worth the purchase price, our [InBody alternatives guide](/blog/best-inbody-alternatives/) covers other ways to maintain a progress record. Buying a scale is only useful when you will follow the routine it requires.

## What are H30's main strengths and limitations?

H30 covers the core recurring check-in: step on, use the handle as instructed, then review your own history. Its US listing includes a splash-resistant LCD showing weight, percent body fat, skeletal muscle mass and visceral fat level. Those are useful conveniences when you want a consistent record without adding a separate appointment.

The limitation is what you can infer from the outputs. A whole-body muscle estimate cannot tell you whether your chest, back or quadriceps have become stronger. And the shared eight-electrode format should not be treated as proof that this home device is interchangeable with whichever professional InBody you used at a gym.

Choose it when your goal is a regular record and the extra H40 fields are unlikely to affect your review. That leaves more budget for other parts of your training rather than a screen you rarely open.

## What does H40 add for someone who lifts?

H40's US listing emphasizes body-balance evaluation and lower-body muscle information. These outputs are relevant if you want to inspect regional patterns rather than only a whole-body estimate.

The practical question is what happens after you see a difference. You might compare it with repeated single-leg exercise performance or review your longer training history. You should also check that the pattern persists under consistent measurement conditions before interpreting a one-off label.

A balance category does not diagnose an injury, establish the cause of pain or measure force production. If what you want is a strength assessment, exercise performance and an appropriate professional assessment answer that question more directly.

## Is H40 more accurate than H30?

This comparison does not establish that. Feature count, a higher price and additional regional outputs cannot demonstrate a smaller body-fat error for you. Both products use bioelectrical impedance, and the manufacturer's preparation guidance emphasizes consistent conditions.

InBody makes broader accuracy claims on its product pages. To compare these exact models, we would need model-specific evidence using the same reference method and comparable participants, including individual agreement results. We have not independently validated that comparison here.

Keep one measurement method consistent when reviewing a training phase. If you switch devices, mark the date and establish a new baseline. The [smart-scale accuracy guide](/blog/are-smart-scales-accurate/) explains why a different percentage does not by itself prove that the new device is better.

## What should you verify before ordering either model?

Buy the version intended for your region and confirm the seller's current terms. The US and European storefronts display different prices and warranty periods, so a cheaper imported listing is not automatically the same purchase.

- Check the exact model name, regional app support and seller.
- Read the current return and warranty terms.
- Confirm which metrics you want on the scale versus in the app.
- Check any integration field you rely on rather than assuming the whole report exports.
- Read the model's setup, preparation and use restrictions.
- Decide where you will record changes in devices or measurement conditions.

For your first check-ins, follow the manufacturer's routine on a hard, level surface with the intended hand and foot contact. Avoid building a comparison from a relaxed morning reading and a post-workout reading on another day.

## How do you keep the scale result connected to visible progress?

Put your longer scale history beside training performance, waist measurements and consistent photographs. Each contributes different context. When they disagree, investigate the record before changing your program to satisfy a single metric.

The [free progress-photo comparison tool](/tools/progress-photo-compare/) lets you place two check-ins side by side. It can help you review appearance, but photos cannot validate an InBody result or directly measure muscle mass. First, choose the model for the information you will use. Second, set up the same measurement routine each time. Third, evaluate several progress records together before deciding whether your training needs to change.

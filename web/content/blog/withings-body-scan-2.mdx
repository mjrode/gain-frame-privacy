## What does BodyScan 2 add over the original Body Scan?

The [official comparison FAQ](https://support.withings.com/hc/en-us/articles/42653646388625-BodyScan-2-Frequently-asked-questions-FAQ) describes a broader impedance-frequency range, a screen in the handle, and additional cardiovascular and metabolic insights. Some advertised features remain marked as coming soon. Buying the hardware does not establish that every advertised feature is available in your region today.

## How much does BodyScan 2 cost to own?

The [US product page](https://www.withings.com/en-us/collections/bodyscan-2) lists BodyScan 2 at **$599.95**, checked October 15, 2026. That is the hardware price. Tax, promotions and optional services can change the amount you pay.

Separate the checkout into three questions: what the scale costs, which measurements the purchase includes, and which insights require Withings+. The support FAQ identifies subscription-dependent intelligence features and marks some features as forthcoming. Check the current offer for the account and region you will actually use.

A useful budget calculation is **hardware + any chosen subscription + any accessories you need**. If you decide against the optional subscription, confirm that your intended workflow still works. Compare the alternatives in our [body-composition scale guide](/blog/best-body-composition-scales/) before paying for features outside your goals.

## Which features matter for lifting progress?

Start with the question you want to answer. A weight trend helps you follow changes in total mass. A segmental composition estimate may give you another series to watch. Training logs show what you can perform, while repeat photos show visible changes under the conditions you captured.

## Can BodyScan 2 sync with Apple Health?

The Withings app supports Apple Health sharing. Its [official setup instructions](https://support.withings.com/hc/en-us/articles/203728916-Linking-my-Withings-account-to-Apple-Health) explain how to activate sharing and select data permissions. Check those permissions and inspect the resulting record in Health after setup.

Do not assume every proprietary score appears in another app. Confirm the specific data type you need, which app wrote it, and whether the timestamp matches your weigh-in. Our [Apple Health scale guide](/blog/best-smart-scales-apple-health/) explains how to choose a scale around that handoff.

## How should you interpret accuracy claims?

Withings advertises strong DEXA correlation and identifies an internal comparative study in its product-page footnote. Correlation describes how measurements move together across a sample. It does not tell you that your next body-fat reading will be within one percentage point of DEXA.

A useful accuracy report specifies the model, sample, reference method and individual differences. Check those details before transferring a result from the original Body Scan to BodyScan 2. This article does not contain an independent device comparison, so it cannot establish an accuracy winner.

You can try a [photo-based body-fat estimate](/tools/body-fat-from-photo/) as a separate visual reference. Treat that output as an estimate too. Agreement between two consumer estimates does not validate either one.

## What should you check before buying?

- Confirm the product name says BodyScan 2, including on a retailer's checkout and return policy.
- List the two or three features you expect to use every week.
- Check whether each is available now, in your country, with your selected subscription.
- Confirm the Apple Health data types you need.
- Read the manufacturer's suitability and measurement instructions before using electrical-impedance features.
- Keep a baseline photo and record the date if you switch devices.

Choose your tracking routine first, price the required hardware and services second, then review the return terms. A clear routine makes the purchase easier to evaluate after the first few weeks.

## What is the difference between a photo scanner and a 3D scanner?

A photo-first workflow analyzes an image. A model-first workflow builds a representation of your shape and may estimate circumferences or composition from it. Both involve estimates. A realistic avatar does not mean the app has directly measured fat, muscle or internal tissue.

## Are body-fat scanner apps more accurate than smart scales?

This comparison does not contain a controlled app-versus-scale trial, so it cannot establish a general winner. Smart scales estimate composition using impedance; camera apps estimate it from visual information and their models. Each has different inputs and possible error sources.

Photos are also affected by pose, camera position, lighting, clothing and changes in appearance. They are not immune to hydration-related appearance changes. Keep the original images so you can judge whether the scene changed before interpreting an estimated percentage.

## How can you make the record useful over time?

- Use the same app and capture instructions for a series of check-ins.
- Mark changes of app, device or capture setup in your notes.
- Compare similarly framed photos or model captures.
- Check history and export access before paying for a longer plan.
- Use a qualified professional when the result is needed for a medical decision.

Choose one workflow, record a consistent baseline, and review later dates together. You can use the [photo comparison tool](/tools/progress-photo-compare/) to inspect a matching pair without declaring either estimated percentage exact.

### Can a 3D scanner also estimate body-fat percentage?

<p>Some current 3D scanning apps include estimated body-fat percentage alongside avatars and circumferences. Check the individual listing because features change. ### Can a phone app measure body fat directly?

<p>A phone app estimates body composition from images and any information you provide. It does not directly observe every tissue in your body. Keep the original images and standardize capture conditions so you can inspect visible changes alongside the estimates, rather than relying on one precise-looking number.</p>

### Does a high App Store rating prove accuracy?

<p>No. A Store rating reflects reviews from customers who chose to rate the app. It does not compare those customers with a reference measurement under a controlled protocol. Use ratings to investigate usability and support, then evaluate accuracy evidence separately for the particular app and method.</p>

### Is a free download the same as free analysis?

<p>No. An app can be free to install while charging for a report, history or advanced analysis. Check the current purchase screen before subscribing.

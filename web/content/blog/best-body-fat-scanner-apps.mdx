You download a "body fat scanner," point your phone at the mirror, and it spits out a number. The next app gives you a number three points lower. A smart scale gives you a fourth number that changes after you drink a glass of water.

So which body fat scanner can you actually trust? We tested the apps that scan body fat percentage straight from a photo — no calipers, no electrodes, no $150 appointment — and ranked them by accuracy, consistency, and what you get beyond a single number.

One note up front: every app here produces an *estimate* from a photo, not a direct measurement like DEXA. The best ones get impressively close. The worst ones guess.

## 1\. [GainFrame](https://gainframe.app) — Most accurate AI body fat scanner

**Platform:** [iOS App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gainframe-progress-photos/id6759252082) · **Price:** Free with Pro subscription

![GainFrame check-in screen showing an AI body fat scan result of 17%, body weight, and a 0–100 GainFrame Score broken into body-fat, muscle, and proportion sub-scores](../ai-fitness-analysis/assets/post-check-in-photo-score.webp)

GainFrame works as a camera-based body fat scanner — point your phone, take a photo, and its AI estimates your body fat percentage from what it sees. But where most scanners stop at one number, GainFrame keeps going. Every scan gets a "Deep Dive": estimated body fat %, BMI, FFMI, a composite physique score (0–100), and individual ratings for 12 muscle groups.

What separates it from a single-scan app is longitudinal tracking. GainFrame builds a timeline of your body fat and score across every photo you scan, and when you compare two scans side by side it quantifies exactly what changed — body fat delta, FFMI delta, which muscle groups improved. Then it generates training and nutrition recommendations from those changes.

![GainFrame side-by-side comparison showing a before/after muscle body map shifting from red to green, alongside a radar chart that scores 12 individual muscle groups](../how-to-tell-if-skinny-fat/assets/muscle-compare.webp)

The accuracy claim is backed by a [comparison against a clinical DEXA scan](../dexa-scan-vs-ai-body-composition/) — GainFrame scanned 19% on a user whose DEXA measured 18.6%. That's within 0.4%, better than most smart scales manage on a good day. It's still an estimate, but it's the closest any photo scanner got in our testing.

A 2026 update added an **AI Coach** you can chat with about any scan. It's grounded in your actual numbers — your body fat estimate, GainFrame Score, and weight trend — so when you ask "why did my score drop?" the answer references your real data, not generic tips.

![GainFrame AI Coach chat screen — the coach already knows the user's 17% body fat, score, and weight, with suggested questions like "Why did my score drop?" and "What's my biggest area for improvement?"](../aesthetic-physique-body-fat-percentage/assets/ai-coach.webp)

**Strengths:** DEXA-validated accuracy on a single photo, 12 muscle group scoring, longitudinal tracking with comparison reports, daily macro targets, and a data-grounded AI Coach. More data per scan than anything else on this list.

**Weaknesses:** iOS only. The depth means there's more to read — if you only ever want one number and nothing else, it's more app than you need.

**Best for:** People who want a body fat scanner whose numbers actually mean something over time, not a one-off guess.

**Try it without downloading:** Our [free browser-based body fat scanner](/tools/body-fat-from-photo/) runs the same AI engine in single-photo mode — one scan per day, no signup. Good for testing the method before committing to the iOS app.

## 2\. [Formfy](https://apps.apple.com/app/formfy-ai-body-fat-scanner/id6468893498) — Fastest single-scan body fat scanner

**Platform:** [iOS App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/formfy-ai-body-fat-scanner/id6468893498) · **Price:** Free with subscription

Formfy is built for speed. Take a full-body photo and the AI returns a body fat estimate within seconds. It also bundles a meal photo scanner for calorie counting, making it a dual-purpose tool.

**Strengths:** The instant scan is genuinely fast and the interface is clean. If you want a quick number with minimal friction, Formfy delivers.

**Weaknesses:** Formfy pushes you to rate the app immediately after your first scan — before you can judge accuracy. Some features are gated behind a hidden requirement to use the app for 7 consecutive days. There's no timeline, no side-by-side comparison, and no muscle group analysis. You get a snapshot number with no way to see whether it's trending the right way.

**Best for:** People who want a one-off scan and don't need long-term tracking.

<hr className="post-divider" />

## 3\. [bodyfatAI](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bodyfatai-body-fat-scanner/id6751823335) — Simplest photo scanner

**Platform:** [iOS App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bodyfatai-body-fat-scanner/id6751823335) · **Price:** Free with subscription

bodyfatAI does exactly what its name says — it scans body fat and muscle mass from your physique photos and tracks them over time, no calipers or manual measurements. It's the most stripped-down option here, which is the point.

**Strengths:** Dead simple. Snap a photo, get body fat and muscle estimates, watch the number over time. No learning curve.

**Weaknesses:** No published accuracy validation against a clinical reference, so you're trusting the number on faith. Light on the surrounding context — no muscle-group breakdown, no comparison reports, no coaching. It scans and stores; it doesn't interpret.

**Best for:** People who want a no-frills body fat scan and a basic running log of the number.

<hr className="post-divider" />

## 4\. [Spren](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/spren-body-composition-scanner/id1664503999) — Body composition scanner

**Platform:** [iOS App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/spren-body-composition-scanner/id1664503999) · **Price:** Free with subscription

Spren positions itself as a body composition scanner, estimating body fat and BMI from photos and marketing results as comparable to DEXA. It leans more clinical/wellness than gym-aesthetic, and its tech also licenses into other health apps.

**Strengths:** Clean composition-focused scan with body fat and BMI. The brand puts real emphasis on validation, which most photo scanners skip entirely.

**Weaknesses:** Less oriented toward serious lifters — no per-muscle-group scoring or aesthetic physique breakdown. Tracking is lighter than a dedicated progress-photo tool, and "comparable to DEXA" is a marketing claim you should verify against your own baseline.

**Best for:** People who want a composition-first scan and care more about body fat and BMI than muscle-group detail.

<hr className="post-divider" />

## 5\. [Recomp AI](https://apps.apple.com/app/recomp-ai-body-scanner/id6479535498) — Best for recomposition focus

**Platform:** [iOS App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/recomp-ai-body-scanner/id6479535498) · **Price:** Free with subscription

Recomp AI is a body scanner built specifically for people losing fat and gaining muscle at the same time. It scans body fat and lean mass and supports nutrition tracking around the recomp use case.

**Strengths:** The recomp framing speaks to the right user, and estimating both body fat and lean mass gives you both sides of the equation.

**Weaknesses:** No long-term photo timeline, no side-by-side comparison, no workout integration. It's oriented around individual scans rather than a tracked transformation — today's number, without the context of where it's heading.

**Best for:** People mid-recomp who want fat and lean-mass scans without broader tracking.

<hr className="post-divider" />

## 6\. [MeThreeSixty](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/methreesixty-3d-body-scan-bmi/id1472541261) — Best 3D body scanner

**Platform:** [iOS App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/methreesixty-3d-body-scan-bmi/id1472541261) · **Price:** Free with subscription

MeThreeSixty is a different kind of scanner. Instead of estimating a body fat percentage, it uses two photos — front and side — to build a 3D body avatar and capture 14+ circumference measurements. It's a *shape and size* scanner more than a *body fat* scanner.

**Strengths:** The 3D avatar and circumference capture are genuinely useful for tracking waist, hips, arms, and chest over time without a tape measure. Great for visualizing shape change.

**Weaknesses:** Body fat percentage isn't its focus, so if that's the number you came for, it's a secondary output. The 3D model is the headline feature, not composition accuracy.

**Best for:** People who care about measurements and a 3D shape model more than a precise body fat percentage.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the most accurate body fat scanner app in 2026?

GainFrame is the most accurate body fat scanner app in 2026. It scans your body fat percentage from a single photo, and on one test it estimated 19% on a user whose clinical DEXA scan measured 18.6% — within 0.4%. Unlike smart scales that swing with hydration, photo-based scanning stays consistent day to day.

### Can a phone app really scan your body fat from a photo?

Yes. Body fat scanner apps like GainFrame use a vision AI model to read muscle definition, body proportions, and visible fat distribution in a photo and estimate body fat percentage. It's an estimate from inference, not a direct measurement like DEXA or calipers — but when tested against a clinical DEXA scan, GainFrame landed within 0.4%.

### Are body fat scanner apps more accurate than smart scales?

In most cases, yes. Smart scales use bioelectrical impedance (BIA), which is heavily affected by hydration and can swing 3–5% in a single day. A photo-based body fat scanner reads visual indicators like muscle separation and proportions, which don't change with a glass of water — so readings are more consistent over time.

### Is there a free body fat scanner app?

Yes. GainFrame offers a free tier on iOS that scans body fat percentage, FFMI, BMI, and 12 muscle group scores from a photo. You can also try its [free browser-based body fat scanner](/tools/body-fat-from-photo/) — one scan per day, no signup — before downloading the app.

### What is the difference between a body fat scanner and a 3D body scanner app?

A body fat scanner estimates your body fat percentage from a photo (GainFrame, Formfy, bodyfatAI). A 3D body scanner like MeThreeSixty builds a 3D avatar and captures circumference measurements from front and side photos — it tracks your shape and size, but not body fat percentage accuracy. Choose based on whether you care about fat percentage or measurements.

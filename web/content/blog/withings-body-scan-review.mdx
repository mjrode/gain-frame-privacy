A scale with a handle can cost several hundred dollars, yet its body-fat number can still move between readings. The useful buying question is which features you will use regularly and whether their limits are clear.

This guide covers the **original Withings Body Scan**, based on published specifications and support documents checked October 15, 2026. It is a researched evaluation without an original hands-on accuracy trial. For the successor, see [what changes with BodyScan 2](/blog/withings-body-scan-2/).

## What does the original Withings Body Scan do?

It combines a scale platform with a retractable handle for segmental impedance measurements. The [manufacturer's measurement instructions](https://support.withings.com/hc/en-us/articles/10970533213969-Body-Scan-Measuring-Segmental-Body-Composition) describe hand positioning, barefoot contact and holding still. The extra capture requirements are part of the ownership experience.

Its broader offering includes health features whose availability and intended use depend on region. Confirm the actual functions in the current regional listing rather than assuming every international review describes your device. The original and BodyScan 2 have separate feature sets.

## How should you interpret the body-composition readings?

Withings' [original-model measurement FAQ](https://support.withings.com/hc/en-us/articles/12434342485265-Body-Scan-Frequently-asked-questions-about-Segmental-Body-Composition-measurements) identifies hydration, skin contact and measurement conditions as sources of variation. Follow the device instructions and keep the routine consistent. Extra electrodes do not remove those requirements.

A body-fat percentage on a screen is an estimate. Manufacturer study claims need to be read alongside their protocol and individual differences. This article does not establish that Body Scan is universally more accurate than every cheaper scale or that any one reading equals a DEXA result.

If comparing methods is your main concern, start with our [body-composition measurement guide](/blog/every-way-to-measure-body-fat/). Mark the date whenever you change devices and avoid stitching different methods into one uninterrupted series.

## What does the original model cost, and is Withings+ required?

Withings' current [US scale comparison on the BodyScan 2 page](https://www.withings.com/en-us/collections/bodyscan-2) listed the original Body Scan at $499.95 when checked October 15, 2026. Retail prices and promotions may differ. Confirm that a seller's price is for the original model, then inspect the final checkout.

Treat optional Withings+ services as a separate purchase decision. Check which reports or health services need a subscription and what happens after any trial. The [current generation-comparison FAQ](https://support.withings.com/hc/en-us/articles/42653646388625-BodyScan-2-Frequently-asked-questions-FAQ) distinguishes hardware and subscription features, but regional eligibility still matters.

## Does the original Body Scan work with Apple Health?

The Withings app supports Apple Health sharing. Follow its [setup instructions](https://support.withings.com/hc/en-us/articles/203728916-Linking-my-Withings-account-to-Apple-Health), choose the data permissions you want, then inspect a new reading in Health. Check both its source and timestamp.

This is particularly useful when you care about one reliable weight series across apps. Confirm the specific types you need instead of assuming proprietary scores will transfer. The [Apple Health scale guide](/blog/best-smart-scales-apple-health/) compares this part of the workflow.

## Who should consider the original Body Scan?

Consider it if the original model's confirmed features fit your routine and its total price makes sense next to the alternatives. A buyer who mainly wants a weight trend should compare simpler scales first. Someone considering a successor should identify the new feature they would use regularly.

Before buying, confirm the generation, the regional feature set, subscription needs and return terms. Then establish a consistent measurement routine and save a baseline. Review several dates together before deciding whether the extra information is useful.

### Does a handle guarantee a more accurate body-fat estimate?

<p>No individual accuracy guarantee follows from the presence of a handle. The arrangement changes how impedance is measured, but device algorithms and measurement conditions also matter. Read evidence for the exact model and examine individual differences against the reference rather than relying on hardware design alone.</p>

### Can hydration affect Body Scan measurements?

<p>Yes. Withings identifies hydration and measurement conditions among the reasons segmental estimates can vary. Follow the instructions for your device and use a consistent routine. Avoid treating a single change as newly gained muscle or lost fat, especially when the capture conditions also changed.</p>

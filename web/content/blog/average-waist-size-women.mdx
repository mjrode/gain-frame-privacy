If your jeans fit but the measurement on your tape looks unfamiliar, check what the two numbers describe. A US dress size, a garment waistband and a survey waist measurement each use a different reference.

## Where should you put the tape to measure your waist?

1. Stand upright and find the top edge of your right hip bone directly below the middle of your armpit. Place the tape just above that edge. Your navel can sit above or below this level.
2. Wrap the tape around your bare waist at the same height. A mirror or a helper can check the tape across your back. Let it touch the skin without squeezing.
3. Breathe out normally and read the tape. Record the number, unit and landmark so you can repeat the measurement.

## What is the average waist size for women by age?

NHANES examiners measured 5,065 US women aged 20 and older in 2015 to 2018. The table shows the survey-weighted mean and median by age, converted from centimeters to inches and rounded to one decimal. Pregnant participants were excluded. [NCHS Table 19](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/100478)

| Age in years | Mean waist | Median waist |
|---|---:|---:|
| All adults 20 and older | 38.7 in | 38.0 in |
| 20 to 29 | 36.3 in | 35.0 in |
| 30 to 39 | 38.3 in | 37.1 in |
| 40 to 49 | 39.2 in | 37.8 in |
| 50 to 59 | 39.3 in | 38.5 in |
| 60 to 69 | 40.0 in | 39.4 in |
| 70 to 79 | 40.0 in | 39.6 in |
| 80 and older | 38.7 in | 38.7 in |

The median is the midpoint. About half the group measured below it and half above. For women aged 30 to 39, for example, 37.1 inches was the midpoint and 38.3 inches was the average. Different women contributed to each age group, so the rows cannot predict how one woman's waist will change as she ages.

## Why doesn't your waist measurement match your clothing size?

Look for two things in a brand's chart: where it wants the tape and whether its numbers describe your body or the finished garment. A label such as “size 8” is a size in that brand's system. A low-rise waistband can also sit well below the spot the chart tells you to measure.

If the instructions say “natural waist,” use the location shown by the brand. If the chart asks for hips as well, measure at its specified hip landmark. Keep those readings separate from your NHANES waist measurement. For more on the second measurement, see the [women's hip size guide](/blog/average-hip-size-women/).

## What can a waist measurement tell you about health?

[NIDDK](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/weight-management/adult-overweight-obesity/am-i-healthy-weight) lists a waist of 35 inches or more in women as one indicator of increased risk for obesity-related health problems. The survey mean answers a different question: how large waists were in the sampled population. Neither reading gives a diagnosis.

If you're using a [waist-to-height ratio](/blog/waist-to-height-ratio/), follow the measuring instructions attached to that guidance. A tape does not directly measure the [fat around internal organs](/blog/visceral-fat-level/). A health professional can put the measurement in context if you're concerned about your own result.

## What else should you know about waist measurements?

### What is the average waist size for a woman in the US?

In the 2015 to 2018 US survey, the mean waist circumference for women aged 20 and older was 98.4 centimeters, or 38.7 inches. The median was 96.4 centimeters, or 38.0 inches. Those figures describe measurements taken at the survey's specific hip-bone landmark during those years.

### Where should a woman measure her waist?

Choose the landmark that matches the reason for measuring. To compare with the US survey table, find the upper edge of the right hip bone at your side and wrap the tape horizontally just above it. To buy clothes, follow the measuring instructions supplied with that particular garment.

### How tight should the measuring tape be?

Let a flexible tape rest flat against your skin all the way around. Check in a mirror that it stays level across your back and that it leaves no indentation. Stand comfortably, breathe out normally and read the number before you pull the tape away.

### What waist size is a US size 8?

The number on the label does not specify a circumference in inches. Open the size chart for the exact garment and check whether the brand lists body measurements or the dimensions of the finished garment. Take your measurement at the landmark the brand describes, then compare the same units.

### What does a waist measurement of 35 inches mean?

NIDDK lists a waist measurement of 35 inches or more in women as one indicator of increased risk for obesity-related health problems. One reading cannot establish your individual risk or measure visceral fat. A clinician can interpret it alongside your history and other health measurements.

## Which body scan app is best in 2026?

Choose **MeThreeSixty** for free tape-free measurements, **ZOZOFIT** for a polished 3D scanning ecosystem, and **Progress** if you trust a soft tape more than a camera estimate. Choose **GainFrame** if you searched for a body scanner because you want to know whether your training is visibly working. It tracks body fat, FFMI, and muscle-group change from real progress photos.

That distinction matters. A circumference scanner can tell you that your waist estimate moved. A photo tracker can show whether you look leaner, where muscle definition changed, and whether the same shift appears across several check-ins.

## What is the difference between a body scan app and a body measurement app?

The App Store mixes three different products under the same search:

- **3D body scanners** such as MeThreeSixty, ZOZOFIT, and Recomp AI reconstruct a body model and estimate circumferences from camera input.
- **Manual measurement trackers** such as Progress and My Body Measurement Tracker organize numbers you take with a soft tape.
- **AI photo trackers** such as GainFrame analyze real progress photos for body fat, FFMI, muscle development, and visible change.

The right category depends on the question. "Did my waist shrink?" is a measurement question. "Did this cut improve my physique?" is a photo and body-composition question.

[GainFrame](https://gainframe.app) does not pretend to measure your chest or waist from a photo. It answers the nearby question that matters more to many lifters: **does my body actually look different?** Each check-in adds body fat percentage, BMI, FFMI, a physique score, and ratings for 12 muscle groups to the original progress photos.

Photos stay in the on-device library. Selected images are securely processed when you request AI analysis and are not stored on GainFrame servers. No account is required to start.

**Best for:** iPhone users tracking a cut, bulk, or recomp who care about visible change. **Limitations:** no circumference estimates, iOS only, and every body-composition number is an estimate rather than a direct measurement.

## How accurate are body scanning apps compared with a tape measure?

Phone scans are accurate enough to reveal broad shape and circumference trends when the setup is consistent. They are less dependable when clothing, posture, camera height, distance, or lighting changes. A generated avatar can also look smooth even when the underlying estimate moves.

A soft tape has its own error. Pulling harder, measuring a slightly different point, or taking a waist reading after a meal can shift the result. Repeat each measurement twice, use the same anatomical landmarks, and measure at the same time of day.

For the strongest low-cost system, combine the two signals: one or two tape measurements each month plus standardized progress photos. The tape shows inches. The photos show leanness, muscle definition, and proportion. Our [body measurement guide](/blog/how-to-take-body-measurements/) covers the exact protocol. The free [AI body proportion calculator](/tools/body-measurements-calculator/) analyzes one front-facing photo, scores visible balance, highlights your strongest area and biggest opportunity, and lets you adjust shoulders, chest, waist, arms, or legs for an optional AI preview. It does not accept tape measurements or calculate exact circumferences.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best body scan app in 2026?

MeThreeSixty is the best free 3D body scan app. ZOZOFIT is the most polished scanning ecosystem. GainFrame is the better fit when the goal is to compare visible fat and muscle change rather than estimated circumferences.

### What is the best body measurement app?

Progress is our top manual measurement tracker. It combines tape measurements, weight, BMI, body fat, and progress photos, has been maintained since 2012, and had more than 9,300 US ratings when checked.

### Do body scanning apps work on Android?

MeThreeSixty and ZOZOFIT support Android and iOS. GainFrame, Recomp AI, and My Body Measurement Tracker are iOS-only. Android users can also try GainFrame's [browser body fat estimator](/tools/body-fat-from-photo/) without installing an app.

### Can a phone scan body measurements accurately?

It can estimate measurements well enough for broad trends, but it is sensitive to setup. Use the same clothing, posture, camera position, and lighting each time, and confirm important numbers with a tape.

### Is a 3D body scan better than progress photos?

A 3D scan is better for estimated circumferences and shape. Progress photos are better for definition, leanness, proportions, and seeing your real appearance. Many lifters should use both.

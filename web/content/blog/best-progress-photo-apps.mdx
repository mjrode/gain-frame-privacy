## Which app fits photo-based physique tracking?

### 1. GainFrame: physique estimates alongside your photo history

[Official site](https://gainframe.app) · [US App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gainframe-progress-photos/id6759252082) · **4.89/5 from 62 ratings** (September 21, 2026).

<div className="post-render-figure"><img src="assets/gainframe-app.webp" alt="Dated side-by-side check-ins with physique scores and estimated body-fat context." loading="lazy" /><p className="post-caption">App screenshot from our library. Dated side-by-side check-ins with physique scores and estimated body-fat context.</p></div>

The app combines imports, guided poses, dated comparisons and photo-based body-fat estimates. Detailed muscle analysis covers 12 groups across the relevant poses. Hevy integration adds training context when you choose to connect it.

Automatic scoring has free access within capture and import limits. Advanced Deep Dive reports, AI Compare and unlimited imports require Pro. Limits differ between newer and legacy installs, so the allowance shown in your app is the relevant one.

**Choose it for:** repeated physique check-ins with written analysis available on Pro. **Tradeoff:** iOS only, and the estimates depend on the submitted photos. The library stays on your device; selected photos are processed by an AI service when you request analysis.

## Which app combines measurements with paid photo tracking?

### 2. Progress by Lasmit: measurements and photo context

[Official site](https://theprogressapp.com) · [US App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/body-measurements-tracker-app/id583840813) · **4.63/5 from 9,344 ratings** (September 21, 2026).

The US listing currently calls this Body Measurements Tracker App. It supports weight and measurement records, side-by-side photos and Apple Health integration. Its release history describes Smart Overlay and photo-date navigation.

**Choose it for:** a measurement-led record with photos beside the numbers. **Tradeoff:** the listing explicitly puts progress photos, past-data entry and several reporting features in Pro. Logging body-fat percentage should not be confused with estimating it from a photo.

## Which app keeps progress photos beside your training log?

### 5. Hevy: private photos and workout context

[Official site](https://www.hevyapp.com) · [US App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hevy-workout-tracker-gym-log/id1458862350) · **4.92/5 from 93,597 ratings** (September 21, 2026).

<div className="post-render-figure"><img src="assets/hevy-app.webp" alt="Hevy records training sessions; its separate Measures area holds private progress photos." loading="lazy" /><p className="post-caption">Developer-supplied App Store screenshot. [Hevy's photo documentation](https://www.hevyapp.com/features/progress-photos/) describes camera or library uploads through Profile, then Measures. You can browse dates, compare two photos side by side and share an image. These private progress photos are separate from photos you post with workouts.

**Choose it for:** keeping a visual record in an app you already use for training. **Tradeoff:** the guide documents one progress photo per day, which is awkward for a front, side and back set. Its [free-versus-Pro guide](https://help.hevyapp.com/hc/en-us/articles/35119778922263-Hevy-Pro-Subscription-How-to-get-Pro-and-What-Does-It-Include) lists limits for routines, data history and advanced tracking; it does not provide a complete photo-feature allowance table.

## How do you choose without rebuilding your photo library twice?

- Import two existing photos before committing to a large migration.
- Check whether the original dates remain clear.
- Try the alignment and comparison view you intend to use.
- Export a sample and check its resolution, watermark and price.
- Confirm where the photos are stored and how to retrieve them.

First, choose the workflow you need. Second, capture [repeatable progress-photo poses](/blog/progress-photo-poses/). Third, try a comparison with your own files. You can [compare two progress photos in your browser](/tools/progress-photo-compare/) before choosing a long-term app.

## What else should you know?

### What is the best app for progress photos?

<p>The best fit depends on your workflow. A photo-analysis app suits repeated physique estimates, Progress combines measurements with paid photo features, Metamorph emphasizes aligned sequences, and Hevy adds a private photo record to workout tracking. Compare import, export and free limits using two photos before choosing a long-term library.</p>

### Are progress photo apps really free?

<p>Some offer usable free features, while others charge for imports, comparisons or photo history. A zero-price download alone does not establish what you can keep using without payment. For example, Progress lists photos as a Pro feature and SnapTrack lists imports and overlays as separate purchases.</p>

### Which progress photo apps can align images?

<p>Metamorph advertises automatic alignment and overlays, and Progress describes Smart Overlay in its release history. The app made by this publisher also offers alignment and guided poses. Check the current tier required for each feature, and keep your camera position and pose consistent so alignment has comparable inputs.</p>

### Can Hevy compare progress photos side by side?

<p>Yes. Hevy documents a private progress-photo library under Measures, where you can select a photo, position it beside another and create a shareable image. Its guide currently describes one photo per day. That differs from posting a gym photo alongside a workout, which follows your social-profile visibility.</p>

### Can an app tell exactly how much muscle I gained?

<p>A photo app can document visible changes and may estimate body composition, but images alone cannot establish an exact amount of new muscle. Lighting, posture and clothing affect the result. Compare consistent check-ins alongside your training and weight context, and treat the estimates as one part of the record.</p>

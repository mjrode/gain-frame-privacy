A cheap RENPHO bathroom scale and a Hume Body Pod are easy to compare by price, but they are different purchases. If you want a handle-based composition system, the more useful RENPHO comparison is the MorphoScan Nova. This page keeps those two models in view.

**Research basis:** Specifications were checked September 6, 2026. We have not tested these devices side by side. This comparison is written by Michael Rode, founder of GainFrame, which provides photo-based progress tracking. It does not change the findings or testing history of our separate product reviews.

## Which two models are being compared?

The Hume Body Pod and RENPHO MorphoScan Nova both use hand-and-foot contact in their body-composition workflow. RENPHO specifies eight-electrode, dual-frequency BIA for the Nova. Hume markets its Body Pod as a whole-body composition system with a companion app. [Hume Body Pod](https://humehealth.com/products/the-body-pod), [RENPHO MorphoScan Nova](https://renpho.com/products/morphoscan-nova-body-composition-analyzer)

This comparison does not cover Hume's wearable band or every RENPHO scale. If a retailer listing has a different model name, use its own specifications.

## When does the RENPHO Nova make more sense?

The Nova is a clear candidate if seeing readings on a handheld screen is high on your list. Its product page also explicitly advertises app access without subscription fees. Those are tangible purchasing features you can check without accepting every interpretation attached to the report.

Before buying, confirm the initial setup requirements and the role of the RENPHO Health app. An on-device screen does not necessarily mean the entire product works without an account, phone, or initial pairing. Verify the actual workflow you want, especially if buying for someone else.

## When does the Hume Body Pod make more sense?

The Body Pod is worth considering if you already use the Hume ecosystem or prefer its approach to presenting a history. Try to inspect the app screens and distinguish included data from optional paid services before you compare long-term cost.

Hume's pages and campaigns can show different offers. Use the delivered price for the same model, with optional plans and accessories separated. We are not treating an advertised discount percentage as evidence that one device is better value.

For a fuller evaluation of the product itself, use our existing [Hume Body Pod review](/blog/hume-body-pod-review/). This page answers the narrower two-product choice.

## Can either brand claim the accuracy win from these specifications?

The specifications do not support an accuracy ranking. More outputs, a handle, or a correlation claim cannot tell you the expected error in your own body-fat reading. A convincing comparison needs both devices tested under a documented protocol against the same reference assessment.

Even a personal side-by-side test has limits. If one device gives a lower number, that does not establish that it is closer to the reference. If both agree, that alone does not establish that either is correct.

Use the [body-composition methods guide](/blog/every-way-to-measure-body-fat/) if you need the broader explanation. Do not interpret a Hume-versus-RENPHO shopping table as clinical validation.

## What else should you know?

### Which RENPHO scale compares with the Hume Body Pod?

This guide uses the MorphoScan Nova because it also provides a handle-based body-composition workflow. A basic foot-only RENPHO scale is a different purchase with different features. Check the exact name in a listing before using a brand-level comparison to make a decision about hardware or price.

### Is Hume more accurate than RENPHO?

This comparison does not establish an accuracy winner. We have not run a controlled test of these models against the same reference assessment. Sensor descriptions, output counts, and marketing claims cannot substitute for that evidence, and a lower body-fat estimate is not automatically a better one.

### Does RENPHO MorphoScan Nova require a subscription?

RENPHO advertises full app access without subscription fees for the Nova. It uses the RENPHO Health app, and you should check the current setup instructions before purchase. Subscription access and initial phone or account requirements are separate questions, especially if the scale is intended for another household member.

### Does the Hume Body Pod include a free app?

Hume advertises a free companion app with the Body Pod. Before purchase, distinguish the functions included with your device from any optional paid service presented in the offer. Check the specific reports you want rather than assuming every feature shown anywhere in the Hume ecosystem is included.

### Should you keep both Hume and RENPHO readings in one trend?

Keep each device labeled as a separate series. Different systems can estimate composition differently, so a change between brands should not be counted as a physical change. If you replace a scale, mark the switch date and build a new baseline while retaining the old history for context.

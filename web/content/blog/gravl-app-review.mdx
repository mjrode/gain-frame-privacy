## What should you test during your first three sessions?

A short trial can reveal usability problems. It cannot demonstrate long-term muscle growth. Use these three sessions as a proposed evaluation, and adapt them to your established training plan rather than inventing a new program just to test software.

| Session | Real-world situation | What to record |
|---|---|---|
| First | Enter your actual equipment and normal schedule | Does the suggested session fit the time and equipment you have? |
| Second | A planned exercise is unavailable | Can you choose a sensible substitution and retain a clear log? |
| Third | Repeat a movement you previously logged | Can you understand and edit the proposed load and repetitions? |

For each session, record how long setup took, how often you corrected an entry, and whether you understood the next recommendation. Those observations are more useful for your buying decision than a generic promise that an app is personalized.

Do not manufacture pain or exhaustion to test the algorithm. If a recommendation conflicts with your ability, equipment, or professional guidance, change it or skip it. An app cannot watch every repetition you perform.

## How should you judge progression recommendations?

A suggestion to add weight is only useful if you can connect it to what you actually did. Before accepting it, look back at the previous movement, load, repetitions, and any notes you made about execution.

Ask whether you can distinguish a repeated exercise from a substituted one. A machine press and a dumbbell press can have different loading conventions. A larger number after a substitution does not automatically mean you became stronger.

| Question | Why it matters to your purchase |
|---|---|
| Can I correct an accidentally logged set? | The history should reflect the workout you completed |
| Can I understand the next target? | You need a recommendation you can evaluate |
| Can I retain notes about a substitution? | Different equipment can make totals hard to compare |
| Can I finish logging without interrupting the session? | A feature has little value if you stop using it |

These criteria define what to evaluate. We have not established whether Gravl passes each test. Our [strength standards guide](/blog/strength-standards/) supplies broader context if you want to interpret lifting performance; this article stays with choosing the app.

## Which Hume and Withings models does this compare?

This article compares the **original Hume Body Pod**, also shown as Hume Pod in current navigation, with the **original Withings Body Scan**. Their newer products, Hume Pod 2.0 and BodyScan 2, are separate purchase choices. Confirm the exact model on the listing and checkout before applying a feature or price comparison. [Hume product catalog](https://humehealth.com/products/the-body-pod) and [Withings Body Scan](https://www.withings.com/en-us/collections/body-scan)

Withings also sells other scales. A comparison that uses a Body Scan feature alongside a lower-priced model's price will give you a misleading budget. Keep one model name on each side of your shortlist.

## What are the published differences?

Hume emphasizes body-composition tracking through its scale and app, including estimates for body regions and a range of displayed metrics. Withings Body Scan lists muscle and fat across five zones, six-lead ECG and vascular-age features. Withings also advertises Wi-Fi or Bluetooth connectivity and recognition for up to eight users. [Hume Body Pod details](https://humehealth.com/products/the-body-pod) and [Body Scan specifications](https://www.withings.com/en-us/collections/body-scan)

## Do you need a paid app subscription?

Hume's app page promotes a free app alongside paid offerings, and Withings states that baseline metrics and trends remain accessible without Withings+. Separate your required features from optional coaching or interpretation before assessing value. [Hume app information](https://humehealth.com/pages/hume-app) and [Withings subscription FAQ](https://www.withings.com/en-us/collections/body-scan)

Make a short list: recent readings, a longer history, household separation and whatever export or connected-app workflow you use. Ask the manufacturer about any requirement that is unclear. A free download alone does not answer which features remain available after a trial.

## What should you budget for?

The US Withings collection page lists the original Body Scan at **$499.95** when checked September 23, 2026. Hume uses changing offers across its product and campaign pages, so this guide does not assign one universal Hume checkout price. Compare the actual offer for the original model, including shipping, tax, optional memberships and renewal terms. [Withings US pricing](https://www.withings.com/en-us/collections/body-scan) and [Hume's current offer](https://humehealth.com/products/the-body-pod)

Save the exact purchase terms you receive, especially the return window and any conditions for opened hardware. A price comparison is incomplete when one side includes a membership or a different generation of the scale.

## Does a longer metric list prove better accuracy?

No. The count of displayed outputs does not establish independent accuracy. Manufacturer accuracy statements need their underlying study, comparator, participant group and error measures to be interpreted. This article has not independently tested either device and cannot rank them on individual body-fat accuracy.

Treat a switch between scales as a change in your tracking system. Keep a note of the date and avoid assuming a difference between two devices represents a physical change. Follow the chosen manufacturer's setup and measurement instructions consistently.

## How should you evaluate the scale during a return window?

Try the routine you expect to keep: take a reading, confirm it reached the right profile, and find it in the app. Check the workflow for another household member if sharing the scale matters. Then review the history on another day to see whether the information is easy to retrieve.

For a separate visual record, keep progress photos under consistent conditions. A private album or GainFrame can hold that history, and a [photo comparison](/tools/progress-photo-compare/) can make a pair easier to view. Photos provide another record of appearance; they do not independently validate a scale's body-fat estimate.

## What else should you know?

### Is Hume more accurate than Withings?

This article does not establish an accuracy winner. It compares manufacturer documentation and practical purchase considerations, without a controlled test of the two devices. To evaluate an accuracy claim, inspect the study design, comparator, participants and reported errors rather than relying on a headline percentage or number of displayed metrics.

### Are Withings Body Scan and BodyScan 2 the same scale?

No. They are separate models, and this comparison covers the original Body Scan. Check the full model name when comparing prices, health features and regional availability. A newer product page can describe capabilities that should not be attributed to the original device in an older comparison.

### Can you use either scale without paying for premium coaching?

Both manufacturers describe free app access alongside optional paid offerings. Withings explicitly includes baseline metrics and trends without Withings+. Check Hume’s current included features and both companies’ purchase terms for the exact workflow you need, especially if your offer includes a trial that later renews automatically.

### Do progress photos replace a body-composition scale?

They record a different kind of information. A photo preserves appearance under particular conditions, while the scale produces its own measurements and estimates. Keep both records consistent if you use them together, and avoid treating a visual difference as proof that a particular body-fat number is correct.

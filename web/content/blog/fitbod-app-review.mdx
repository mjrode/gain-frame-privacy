You open your workout app with 45 minutes available, but the cable station is occupied and last week's notes live in another screen. This Fitbod app review focuses on that everyday job: turning your equipment, schedule and previous training into a session you can actually run.

Fitbod also has a Body Composition dashboard. It does not create those measurements from your appearance: it displays imported or manually entered values. Understanding that distinction makes the app easier to evaluate, and avoids choosing a workout subscription for a measurement feature it does not claim to provide.

This is a sourced evaluation checked October 27, 2026, using Fitbod's documentation and current US listing. We have not run a controlled training trial. Publisher screenshots illustrate the interface, not results we achieved with the app.

## Who is Fitbod best for in this app review?

Fitbod is most compelling when you want help deciding what to do next. Its [product description](https://fitbod.me/) centers on strength workouts adapted to your goals, experience and available equipment. Someone who already follows a coach's fixed program may place less value on generated sessions than someone juggling a home setup and a commercial gym.

Its usefulness still depends on honest inputs. If you skip an exercise but leave the prescribed sets in the log, the next recommendation is learning from work you did not perform. Treat suggestions as editable starting points, then record what happened.

**Platform:** [iOS App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fitbod-gym-fitness-planner/id1041517543) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.fitbod.fitbod). **US rating checked October 27:** 4.81/5 from 288,317 US ratings.

## How does Fitbod choose exercises and change your workouts?

Fitbod's [exercise-selection guide](https://help.fitbod.me/hc/en-us/articles/43489509474455-How-does-Fitbod-choose-which-exercises-to-give-me) describes equipment, goals, experience, training split, preferences and feedback as inputs. You can influence recommendations rather than repeatedly accepting movements that do not fit your gym. The [current customization guide](https://help.fitbod.me/hc/en-us/articles/38318585683991-Customizing-Today-s-Workout) separates persistent plan settings from changes to today's duration or equipment.

That distinction is useful. A busy gym today should not necessarily rewrite your long-term goal. Set the training plan deliberately, then adjust the day's practical constraints when needed.

Variation is also a tradeoff. New movements can make a session workable or enjoyable, but comparisons become harder if you change exercises constantly. Keep a small set of anchor lifts in your review, even if accessories vary. Our [Fitbod versus Hevy comparison](/blog/fitbod-vs-hevy/) covers the broader choice between these workout workflows.

## Does Fitbod measure body fat or muscle mass?

No. The [Body Composition documentation](https://help.fitbod.me/hc/en-us/articles/18213217448983-Body-Composition) says the displayed measurements come from Apple Health, Android Health Connect or manual entry. Weight, body-fat percentage and lean mass are supported on both platforms. Some fields differ: BMI and waist circumference are documented on iOS, while BMR appears on Android.

A scale estimate does not become a new independent measurement when another app draws its graph. If your body-fat line suddenly shifts, inspect which device supplied it and whether the conditions changed. Manual entries remain in Fitbod rather than automatically writing back to the connected health platform.

## Is the muscle recovery map a body-composition scan?

No. Fitbod's [recovery explanation](https://help.fitbod.me/hc/en-us/articles/43492170863895-How-does-Fitbod-know-my-muscles-are-still-recovering) describes a model based on logged training, with an option to adjust the estimate. It is intended to inform programming. A colored muscle map does not photograph your muscle size or directly measure tissue recovery.

Read the map alongside how a movement feels, your recent sessions and any relevant professional guidance. If the app suggests a movement you cannot perform comfortably, a high recovery percentage is not a reason to force it. The model is useful context rather than an examination of your body.

## How much does Fitbod cost, and what should you test first?

Fitbod's [subscription help](https://help.fitbod.me/hc/en-us/sections/1500000506081-Subscriptions), checked October 27, lists $15.99 USD monthly or $95.99 annually. Region, store and promotions can change the offer. Confirm the trial duration, renewal date and billing terms on your own purchase screen; a free download is not an unlimited free workout log.

The best trial is a miniature version of your normal week, not a tour through every menu. Try the equipment you really have, one familiar exercise, one substitution and the time limit you can actually sustain. Record where you needed to intervene.

Those checks evaluate fit and friction. They cannot demonstrate muscle growth or long-term injury outcomes during a short trial. A subscription is worthwhile if its planning assistance makes your actual training easier to carry out, not because the dashboard contains more numbers.

## How can you connect workouts with visible progress?

Keep the training log and outcome record distinct. The log explains what you did. A dated photo, circumference measurement or weight trend records another aspect of what happened. Start with the [free progress-photo comparison tool](/tools/progress-photo-compare/) if you already have two comparable images.

GainFrame is an optional iOS companion for organizing those pictures and producing photo-based estimates. It is not required to view Fitbod's imported body metrics and is not a direct Fitbod integration. Its photo library stays on-device, while selected photos are sent for AI processing. Use any visual estimate as context, not proof of a precise muscle-mass change.

## What are the main reasons to choose a different workflow?

A fixed coach-written plan may need little algorithmic variation. A lifter who only wants to record sets may prefer a simpler free log. Someone expecting a phone to directly measure body fat needs to reconsider the measurement method, not buy a different workout plan.

Conversely, if planning is what keeps interrupting your consistency, Fitbod addresses a real problem. Compare the alternatives in our [workout tracker guide](/blog/best-workout-tracker-apps/), then choose based on the task you want the app to take over.

## What else should you know before choosing?

### Is Fitbod good for beginners?

<p>Fitbod can help a beginner organize exercises, sets and repetitions around available equipment. That does not replace learning technique or getting individual help when a movement is uncomfortable. Use manageable settings, check the demonstrations and record the work you actually complete. The useful question is whether the plan fits your current abilities and schedule.</p>

### Does Fitbod calculate body-fat percentage?

<p>Fitbod says it does not calculate the body-fat percentage shown in its Body Composition dashboard. The value is imported through a connected health platform or entered manually. If it originally came from a smart scale, the scale remains the source of the estimate. A graph in another app does not independently validate it.</p>

### Is Fitbod free after the trial?

<p>Fitbod offers trials and paid subscriptions rather than an unlimited ongoing free workout log. Its support page says past workouts remain viewable after a trial ends, while logging new workouts requires a subscription. Check the current offer in your region, including renewal timing, because trials and promotions can differ by purchase route.</p>

### Can Fitbod sync with Apple Health or Health Connect?

<p>Yes. Fitbod documents Apple Health on iOS and Health Connect on Android for supported body metrics. The available fields differ between platforms, so check the permissions rather than assuming every metric transfers. Its help page also distinguishes imported information from manually entered data, which is stored in Fitbod without automatically writing back.</p>

### Is Fitbod better than following a fixed workout program?

<p>It depends on the task. Adaptive suggestions can help when equipment, schedule or exercise preferences change frequently. A fixed program can be easier to evaluate when you want deliberate repetition or are following a coach. Neither approach guarantees results. Choose the one you can execute consistently while recording enough detail to review your progress.</p>

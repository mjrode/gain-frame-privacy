  • Toggle Male or Female in the top-right of the card.
  • Drag the Body Fat % slider to step through bands (8–33% male, 18–42% female).
  • Drag the Age Group slider to see how the same body fat reads on different decades.
  • Compare your mirror against the image that feels closest — usually within ±3%.

Why Age & Gender Matter

Same body fat percentage looks different across decades and between sexes. Men lose roughly 3–8% of muscle per decade after 30 (sarcopenia). Women naturally carry 10–13% more essential fat than men and store it in the hips, thighs, and buttocks. A woman at 20% is leaner relative to her gender than a man at 20%.

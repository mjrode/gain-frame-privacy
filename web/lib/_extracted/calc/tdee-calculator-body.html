About This Calculator

This calculator uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, widely considered the most accurate BMR formula for the general population. Your BMR is then multiplied by an activity factor to estimate your TDEE.

How to Use Your TDEE

  • Lose fat: Eat 300–500 calories below your TDEE
  • Maintain: Eat at your TDEE
  • Build muscle: Eat 200–400 calories above your TDEE

These are starting points — adjust based on how your weight responds over 2–3 weeks.

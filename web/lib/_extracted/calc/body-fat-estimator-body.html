About This Calculator

This calculator uses the U.S. Navy body fat formula, which estimates body fat percentage from circumference measurements. It's widely used and reasonably accurate for most people, but all estimation methods have a margin of error (typically ±3–4%). For the most accurate results, measure in the morning before eating.

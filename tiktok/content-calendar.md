## Content Production Notes

### Filming Batch Days
Film 3–5 videos in one session. Batch by type:
- **Text overlay vids** — just need gym b-roll + captions (can use stock)
- **Voiceover explainers** — record audio separately, layer over footage
- **Screen recordings** — capture app moments that tell a story, not a feature tour
- **Reaction/Stitch** — find creators to engage with throughout the week

### Hashtag Sets (rotate these)
- **Body Comp:** `#bodyfat #bodycomposition #bodyrecomposition #naturallifting #gymtok`
- **Progress:** `#progressphotos #transformation #gymgains #fitnessprogress #beforeandafter`
- **Education:** `#fitnesstips #gymtips #workouttips #fitnessadvice #learnontiktok`
- **General:** `#gym #fitness #workout #gains #physique`

### Engagement Tactics
- [ ] Reply to every comment for first 30 min after posting
- [ ] Respond to top comments with follow-up videos
- [ ] Post between 6–9 AM or 5–8 PM EST (peak fitness TikTok times)
- [ ] Pin best-performing video from each week

# GainFrame Analytics: Claude + Rube MCP Cheat Sheet

With the `waitlist_joined` GA4 event and UTM tracking fully configured on `gainframe.app`, you can now query your live analytics directly within Claude using the **Rube MCP** (`google_analytics` toolkit).

Here are the most common natural language commands you can paste into Claude to instantly generate reports. 

*(Note: Claude will automatically map these questions to the `GOOGLE_ANALYTICS_RUN_REPORT` tools under the hood).*

---

## 1. Top Level Conversions
Use these to understand high-level performance and signups.

> *"Use the Rube MCP to tell me how many total `waitlist_joined` events happened on the GainFrame website in the last 7 days."*

> *"Pull a GA4 report showing the total number of sessions vs the total number of `waitlist_joined` events for this month. What is the conversion rate?"*

---

## 2. Marketing Attribution (Where are signups coming from?)
Use these when you want to see which specific Reddit post, Twitter link, or Google search resulted in a Waitlist signup.

> *"Using Rube MCP, run a report showing me the `sessionMedium` and `sessionSource` for every `waitlist_joined` event over the last 30 days."*

> *"I ran a promotion last week. Can you pull the GA4 data showing how many `waitlist_joined` events were generated by the UTM campaign 'spring_launch'?"*

---

## 3. SEO & Content Performance
Use these to determine which of your 11 blog posts are actually driving the most business value.

> *"Run a GA4 pivot report showing me page views grouped by `pagePath`. Then, show me how many `waitlist_joined` events occurred on each of those page paths so I can see which blog post converts best."*

> *"Tell me which landing page (`pageTitle`) had the highest bounce rate over the last 14 days."*

---

## 4. Debugging & Discovery
If you ever forget what dimensions exist, or want to make sure your tracking is working:

> *"Can you list all the available GA4 properties connected to my account using Rube, and confirm the Property ID for the GainFrame Website?"*

> *"What were the top 10 custom events fired on the website yesterday?"* 

---

### Important Things to Remember:
1. **The 24-Hour Delay:** Google Analytics 4 processes standard reports on a 24 to 48 hour delay. If you launch a Reddit post today, do not ask Claude for the report until tomorrow. 
2. **First-Time Setup:** If Claude ever says it cannot connect to GA4, just tell it: *"Call `RUBE_MANAGE_CONNECTIONS` with toolkit `google_analytics`"* and it will give you a link to re-authenticate with Google.

# Typography and Color

## Primary visual system

GainFrame's current product system is light-mode-first, monochrome, and clinical without feeling sterile. Green communicates favorable change and active progress. Red belongs mainly to the logo and rare brand accents.

## Typography

- **Product UI:** Apple's system type / SF Pro behavior.
- **Website and polished marketing display:** Outfit Bold or SemiBold.
- **Website and polished marketing body:** DM Sans Regular, Medium, or SemiBold.
- **Mascot comics / poster headlines:** Impact or Bebas Neue-style condensed all-caps display.
- **Mascot supporting copy:** Helvetica/Arial-style clean sans serif.

The `fonts/` folder contains the Outfit and DM Sans files that ship in the app repository. See `fonts/FONT-NOTICE.txt` before redistributing.

## Color roles

| Role | Hex | Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| Primary ink | `#111111` | Headlines, body text, primary buttons |
| Logo near-black | `#1A1A1A` | Canonical logo geometry only |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards and logo background |
| UI canvas | `#F2F2F7` | App-like backgrounds and quiet sections |
| Mascot cream | `#F5F0EB` | Comic/carousel backgrounds |
| Positive green | `#34C759` | Favorable change, progress, active state |
| Deep positive green | `#1B7A34` | Green text on light backgrounds |
| Logo red | `#E53935` | Canonical logo and mascot bracket accent |
| UI brand red | `#E84C3D` | Rare brand accent outside the logo |
| Secondary text | `#6B7280` | Supporting copy and metadata |
| Divider | `#E5E7EB` | Hairlines and card boundaries |

## Practical combinations

- **Default marketing:** `#FFFFFF` or `#F5F0EB` background, `#111111` text, `#34C759` payoff word.
- **Clinical product layout:** `#F2F2F7` canvas, white cards, black typography, green deltas.
- **Mascot education:** `#F5F0EB`, black illustration/body, olive shorts, `#E53935` bracket and selected word.
- **Dark creative:** use sparingly; keep the logo/mascot legible with a clean white holding area or high-contrast outline. Do not invert/recolor the canonical mark unless a separate approved asset is supplied.

# GainFrame Logo Usage

## Canonical files

- `GainFrame-Mark.svg` - preferred scalable source.
- `GainFrame-App-Icon-1024.png` - production app icon; opaque 1024 x 1024 PNG.
- `GainFrame-Mark-High-Res.png` - large raster mark for layouts.
- `GainFrame-Emoji-128.png` - small social/chat use.

## Colors

- Near-black: `#1A1A1A`
- Red: `#E53935`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

Keep the red lower-right bracket and the open white space inside the frame. Do not recolor, rotate, stretch, outline, add shadows, place text inside the mark, or crop through the frame.

Use clear space of at least 15% of the mark's width on all sides. On photography, use a white or quiet solid-color holding area so the open frame remains legible.

## Wordmark treatment

The repositories do not contain a separate canonical vector wordmark. When a lockup is needed, typeset `GainFrame` as one word in Outfit Bold or a clean bold system sans serif beside the symbol. Keep the symbol visually dominant and do not modify its geometry.

## Do not publish the logo-refresh concepts

The `brand/logo-refresh-*` source folders are explicitly described as direction-setting raster concepts, not production vector artwork. They are not included in this package.

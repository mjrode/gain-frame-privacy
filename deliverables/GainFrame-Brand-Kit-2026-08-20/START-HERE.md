# GainFrame Brand Kit

Prepared for marketing collaborators on August 20, 2026 from the `gain-frame` app repository and the `gain-frame-privacy` website/marketing repository.

## Start here

1. Read `GainFrame-Brand-Profile.pdf` for the concise product, audience, voice, visual system, mascot, and claims guide.
2. Use `01_LOGO/` for canonical logo files. Do not use the July logo-refresh concept folders from the source repository as production marks.
3. Use `02_MASCOT_GARY/` for Gary/GainFrame Guy reference art, poses, animations, and generation templates.
4. Use `03_SCREENSHOTS/CURRENT_FINAL_APP_STORE_3.0/` when you need the repository's current final App Store set.
5. Treat `03_SCREENSHOTS/LATEST_REVIEW_CANDIDATES_3.0/` as newer candidate creative that still needs approval before publishing.
6. Pull source-backed copy and legal guardrails from `05_COPY_AND_CLAIMS/`.

## Status labels used in this kit

- **Canonical** - taken from production app assets or the established brand source.
- **Current final** - explicitly stored as final in the app repository.
- **Review candidate** - newer creative, intentionally separated from final publishing assets.
- **Reference only** - useful as a visual example, not a master template or automatically approved campaign.

## Brand in one sentence

GainFrame turns progress photos into a clear read on what is changing in your physique, then connects that read to coaching, training, recovery, and nutrition context.

## Primary lines

- Positioning lead: **See what's actually changing in your physique.**
- Brand sign-off: **See your gains, frame by frame.**
- App Store name: **GainFrame: Gym Progress Photos**
- App Store subtitle in the repository: **AI Physique & Body Fat Tracker**

## Important publishing checks

- Verify the live App Store price, offer, trial, free-tier limits, and availability immediately before publishing. Active pricing and free-tier experiments exist in the app.
- Label Future You imagery as AI-generated projection. Never imply a guaranteed outcome.
- Describe body-fat, BMI, FFMI, muscle, and macro outputs as AI-generated estimates, not measurements or medical advice.
- Do not say photos never leave the device. The library is stored on-device; selected photos are securely processed when an AI feature is used and are not stored on GainFrame servers.

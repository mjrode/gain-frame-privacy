# Messaging Guide

## Recommended hierarchy

1. **Lead with the outcome:** see what is changing.
2. **Show the proof:** photos, scores, body-fat estimates, muscle groups, or trends.
3. **Explain the next move:** Coach, training, recovery, or nutrition context.
4. **Close with control:** privacy, on-device library, and no account required to start.

## Primary lines

- **Positioning lead:** See what's actually changing in your physique.
- **Brand sign-off:** See your gains, frame by frame.
- **App Store name:** GainFrame: Gym Progress Photos
- **App Store subtitle:** AI Physique & Body Fat Tracker

## Source-backed short descriptions

**25 words**  
GainFrame turns progress photos into body-fat estimates, muscle-group insights, comparisons, and an AI Coach grounded in your real check-ins.

**50 words**  
GainFrame helps gym-goers see what is actually changing. Compare progress photos, track body-fat and physique-score trends, review muscle-group insights, and ask an AI Coach that can use connected training, recovery, and nutrition context to suggest the next move.

## Message pillars and proof

### Progress made visible

- Compare any two check-ins side by side.
- See body-fat, weight, and physique-score change together.
- Build a timeline from existing camera-roll photos.

### Coaching that knows the context

- Coach can use the user's latest check-in, weight trend, training, sleep, and recovery data they choose to connect.
- Guidance should be framed as a focused next move, not generic motivation.

### More than the scale

- Body-fat estimates from photos.
- Physique score, 12 muscle groups, BMI/FFMI trends, Precision estimates, and Future You projections.
- Apple Health, Hevy, and Strava context.

### A complete tracking routine

- Guided check-ins, alignment, import, timeline, streaks, chapters, nutrition logging, and shareable before/after cards.

## Copy style

- Prefer direct verbs: see, know, compare, bring, follow, prove, build.
- Keep headlines short and declarative.
- Use one concrete promise per execution.
- Let screenshots carry detail; do not overload the headline.
- Avoid fear, shame, body judgment, miracle language, or guaranteed outcomes.

## Calls to action

- Start your next check-in.
- See what's changing.
- Turn your photos into proof.
- Bring your progress together.
- Ask Coach what to focus on next.

## Pricing language

Use **Free to start. Upgrade to GainFrame Pro for premium AI and unlimited experiences** only after confirming the current live entitlement wording. Do not hardcode a weekly, monthly, annual, trial, discount, or free-tier limit without a same-day App Store/RevenueCat check.

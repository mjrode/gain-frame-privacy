# Claims and Compliance Guardrails

## Required framing

- GainFrame is a fitness tracking tool, not a medical device.
- AI outputs - including body-fat percentage, BMI, FFMI, muscle scores, physique scores, macro recommendations, and Coach guidance - are estimates or approximations.
- Future You is an AI-generated projection based on the selected inputs, not a prediction guarantee.
- Encourage users to consult a qualified healthcare professional for health or treatment decisions.

## Privacy wording

**Approved direction**

- "Your progress-photo library is stored on your device."
- "No account is required to start tracking."
- "When you choose an AI feature, selected photos are securely processed for analysis and are not stored on GainFrame servers."

**Do not say**

- "Your photos never leave your device."
- "All AI runs on-device."
- "No data is ever sent to a third party."

## Accuracy wording

Use words such as **estimate**, **approximation**, **trend**, **read**, and **signal**. Do not claim clinical accuracy, DEXA equivalence, a fixed error range, or medical validation unless the campaign includes a current approved evidence brief and the exact qualification.

## Competitive wording

Avoid unqualified superlatives such as "the only app," "most accurate," or "no competitor." Prefer specific product facts: camera-roll import, 12 muscle groups, connected Coach context, Future You projection, and progress-photo comparisons.

## Transformation imagery

- Do not imply that an AI-generated physique is an achieved customer result.
- Preserve "AI-generated" or "AI projection" labels on Future You imagery.
- Do not use body-shaming before/after language.
- If a real person appears, confirm the image and its intended channels are already approved for public marketing use.

## Pricing and entitlements

The current app code supports pricing and free-tier experiments. Verify the live localized price, trial, billing period, free usage limits, and Pro benefits before publishing. Never infer the live offer from an old marketing document.

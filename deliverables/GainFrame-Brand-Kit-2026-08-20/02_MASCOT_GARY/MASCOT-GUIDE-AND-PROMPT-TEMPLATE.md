# Gary / GainFrame Guy Mascot Guide

## Character identity

- **Name:** Gary in the app; GainFrame Guy in the social asset library.
- **Head:** four separate black scan-frame corners, an S-curve nose, two expressive googly eyes, and the red lower-right bracket accent.
- **Important:** the head is open. Never draw a solid square or fill behind the eyes/nose.
- **Body:** dark charcoal athletic figure, fitted olive/army-green shorts, compact gray-brown shoes.
- **Default build:** muscular, with broad delts/chest, defined arms and legs, narrow waist, and a clean V-taper.
- **Expression:** carried mainly through the eyes, head/nose angle, pose, and props.

## Use Gary for

- Coaching, explanation, reaction, encouragement, and feature education.
- Visual before/after teaching, exercise demonstrations, progress-photo tips, recovery, nutrition, and measurement concepts.
- A memorable guide beside real UI, data, or product proof.

Avoid using Gary as a substitute for product evidence. The user and their progress remain the hero.

## Production styles in this folder

- `hero-poses/` contains polished transparent PNG/SVG poses for marketing layouts.
- `animated/` contains app animation stills and MOV files.
- `reference-templates/` contains the neutral, muscular, pose, head, badge, and scene references needed to keep new generations consistent.

## Social carousel style

- Canvas: 4:5, normally 1080 x 1350.
- Background: flat clean off-white `#F5F0EB`.
- Illustration: clean comic/cartoon, thick outlines, flat color, restrained shading.
- Cover title: condensed Impact/Bebas Neue-style sans serif, extra bold, all caps.
- Supporting text: clean Helvetica/Arial-style sans serif, bold.
- Highlight one or two words in logo red `#E53935`.
- Cover: title in top 40%, scene in bottom 60%, small GainFrame Guy badge only on the cover.
- Numbered slides: title/subtitle in top 35%, scene in bottom 65%, no badge.

## Reusable image-generation prompt

```text
Create a clean cartoon illustration of the exact GainFrame mascot shown in the attached references.

IDENTITY: The head is not a solid square. It is four separate black corner brackets floating in open space, with the background visible between them. Two googly eyes and an S-curve nose float inside the frame. The lower-right bracket is red (#E53935).

BODY: Match the muscular reference: broad defined deltoids and chest, thick athletic arms, narrow waist, defined legs, fitted olive shorts, and compact gray-brown shoes. Do not soften the body into a round generic character unless the brief explicitly calls for a before/untrained state.

SCENE: [Describe one clear action, prop, or comparison.]

LAYOUT: [4:5 social carousel / square / landscape]. Keep all critical art inside safe margins. Leave intentional clear space for this headline: "[HEADLINE]".

STYLE: Flat clean off-white background (#F5F0EB), thick black outlines, flat colors, minimal shading, no gradients, no extra mascot, no watermark, no random text, no filled square behind the head.
```

Always attach at least the muscular default, head reference/badge, and the closest scene reference. Treat generated text as draft art; re-typeset headlines in a design tool when exact spelling and typography matter.
